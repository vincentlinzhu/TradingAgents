A gamer can easily mix and match:

- Corsair keyboard
- Logitech mouse
- HyperX headset
- ASUS case
- MSI component
- Private-label accessory

There is no meaningful lock-in. This is not Apple, Microsoft, Adobe, or a SaaS platform.

CRSR’s “ecosystem” is mostly product adjacency and brand familiarity. Useful, yes. Deeply protective, no.

That matters because the entire bull case depends on sustaining elevated margins. If competitors discount or launch better-reviewed products, CRSR may need to respond with pricing. That would pressure the margin story fast.

## 10. Macro risk is not a footnote

CRSR is not a core AI infrastructure beneficiary. It is a consumer discretionary hardware stock.

That means it is exposed to:

- Weak consumer spending
- Inflation pressure
- Tighter credit
- Delayed PC upgrade cycles
- Gaming hardware saturation
- Small-cap risk-off sentiment
- Broader tech multiple compression

The macro report was **HOLD**, not BUY. It specifically flagged broad-market valuation risk and small-cap vulnerability. IPO activity does not eliminate that.

If the market turns risk-off, CRSR is exactly the kind of smaller cyclical tech name that can give back gains quickly.
