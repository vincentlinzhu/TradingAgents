# Trading View

## For Existing Long Positions

**Hold**, but manage risk actively.

The trend remains bullish, and there is no confirmed momentum reversal yet. MACD remains above signal, price is above all key moving averages, and VWMA confirms participation. However, RSI above 80 and price being far above the 10 EMA and 50 SMA suggest that upside may now be more vulnerable to profit-taking.

Actionable approach:

- Continue holding while QQQ remains above **695–700**.
- Consider taking partial profits if price spikes toward **720–725** without consolidation.
- Use a trailing stop near **695** for tighter risk control.
- Use a looser trend stop near **681**, the 10 EMA, for those willing to tolerate more volatility.

## For New Long Entries

Avoid chasing aggressively at 711.23.

A better entry would likely come from either:

1. A controlled pullback toward **695–700** that holds as support; or  
2. A deeper pullback toward the **10 EMA near 681**; or  
3. A sideways consolidation above 700 that allows the 10 EMA and VWMA to catch up.

Buying at current levels may work if the rally continues, but the risk/reward is less attractive because QQQ is extended and RSI is extremely overbought.

## For Short Sellers

Shorting QQQ here is high risk.

The trend is strongly bullish, and momentum remains positive. A short setup would require evidence of reversal, such as:

- Failed breakout above 700,
- Daily close below **695**,
- MACD rollover,
- RSI bearish divergence,
- Break below the 10 EMA.

Without those signals, shorting is premature.
