The technical trend is also still clearly positive: QQQ is at a fresh high, above its 10 EMA, 50 SMA, 200 SMA, and VWMA, with MACD still above signal and no confirmed breakdown below the key 695–700 support zone. That said, the bear case is important and should control sizing: QQQ has rallied roughly 27% in six weeks, RSI is very stretched near 83, valuation is elevated at about 34.69x trailing earnings, yield support is minimal, and sentiment has shifted from fear to froth with crowded call activity. So I would not treat QQQ as a table-pounding fresh Buy at current levels. But because leadership, momentum, and the secular growth thesis remain intact, the bear arguments are more about entry discipline and risk control than a reason to move underweight. Net-net, the bull case carries the debate, but only with a staged accumulation plan rather than aggressive chasing.

**Strategic Actions**: For QQQ, maintain a core long position and gradually increase exposure rather than buying a full allocation all at once near 711.23. If currently underweight or unallocated, initiate a partial position of roughly one-third to one-half of the intended QQQ allocation now, then add in tranches on either consolidation above 700 or pullbacks toward 695–700. Reserve additional buying capacity for a deeper reset toward the 10 EMA area near 681 if the broader uptrend remains intact. Do not use aggressive leverage here given the stretched RSI and crowded sentiment. For oversized existing positions, keep the core but consider trimming excess exposure or using collars/protective puts into strength. Use 695–700 as the first tactical risk line; a decisive break below that zone should pause new additions. A break below 681 would warrant reducing tactical exposure, and loss of VWMA near 670–671 would be a stronger warning that the trend is deteriorating. Upside participation should be allowed while QQQ remains above support, with 720–725 and 730 as near-term checkpoints rather than hard sell targets.
