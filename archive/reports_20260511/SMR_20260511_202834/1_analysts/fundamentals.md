## Executive Summary

NuScale Power Corporation (`SMR`) is a development-stage nuclear technology company focused on small modular reactor, or SMR, technology. Fundamentally, `SMR` remains a highly speculative equity: the company has a strong liquidity position following substantial equity issuance, but it continues to generate very limited revenue, large operating losses, negative free cash flow, and severe shareholder dilution.

The balance sheet is currently the company’s strongest fundamental feature. As of 2025-12-31, `SMR` reported approximately **$1.25 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments**, against total liabilities of about **$299.0 million** and minimal non-current liabilities. This provides a meaningful funding runway for product development and commercialization efforts.

However, the income statement and cash flow profile remain weak. Trailing twelve-month revenue was only **$18.7 million**, while net income was approximately **-$385.8 million** and free cash flow was approximately **-$162.0 million** according to the comprehensive fundamentals dataset. Quarterly cash flow data shows that 2025 free cash flow was materially negative, including **-$204.1 million in Q4 2025** and **-$199.9 million in Q3 2025**.

Valuation is difficult to justify on current fundamentals. With a market capitalization of approximately **$4.6 billion** and TTM revenue of only **$18.7 million**, `SMR` trades at an extremely high price-to-sales multiple. The stock is also volatile, with a beta of **2.252**, and has fallen significantly from its 52-week high of **$57.42** to levels much closer to its 52-week low of **$8.85**.

Given the combination of strong liquidity but weak commercial traction, high cash burn, negative earnings, and major dilution, the appropriate fundamental stance is **HOLD** for investors already positioned, while new buyers should wait for clearer evidence of revenue conversion, project awards, regulatory milestones, or sustained cash burn reduction.
