## Trading plan

### For existing long positions

**Bias: Hold.**

QQQ remains technically bullish. The trend is intact across short, medium, and long timeframes. MACD is positive, price is well above key moving averages, and the 50 SMA is above the 200 SMA.

However, because RSI is above 80 and price recently touched the upper Bollinger Band, traders should manage risk more actively.

Possible risk-management approaches:

- Use a **tight tactical stop** below **696** for short-term trades.
- Use a **trend stop** near **687**, around the 10 EMA / 2-ATR downside zone.
- Consider trimming partial profits if QQQ fails again near **713–715**.
- Continue holding core exposure as long as QQQ remains above the rising 10 EMA and MACD stays above its signal line.

### For new long entries

**Avoid chasing at current levels.**

A better entry setup would be one of the following:

1. **Pullback entry:**  
   Look for a controlled pullback toward **687–697**, followed by stabilization or a bullish reversal candle.

2. **Breakout entry:**  
   Consider a breakout only if QQQ closes decisively above **715**, ideally with volume confirmation. Upside targets would be around **724–728** initially.

3. **Momentum reset entry:**  
   If RSI cools from the 80s into the 60s while price holds above the 10 EMA or 50 SMA, the risk/reward would improve.

### For short sellers

Shorting QQQ here is risky because the trend is strongly bullish. A short setup would require confirmation, such as:

- break below **696**,
- failure to reclaim the 10 EMA,
- MACD histogram deterioration,
- and RSI rolling over from extreme levels.

Without that confirmation, bearish trades are counter-trend.
