**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: Both sides agree STM is a much better story than it was at the trough: the balance sheet is strong, Q1 revenue improved year over year, the LEO satellite opportunity could be meaningful, and the company has credible optionality in edge-AI sensors, power semis, microcontrollers, and embedded applications. The bull case is strongest on strategic direction and normalized earnings power: current margins are depressed, forward EPS implies a major recovery, and STM has enough liquidity to invest through the cycle. But the bear case carried the debate on timing and risk/reward. STM has nearly doubled in six weeks and is still trading close to its 52-week high, with RSI above 80, fading MACD momentum, and price far above the 50-day and 200-day averages. Meanwhile, the financial proof is not yet clean: Q1 revenue declined sequentially, gross margin slipped sequentially, operating margin remains low, free cash flow is inconsistent, capex is heavy, and LEO/sensor upside is not yet proven in margins or cash flow. The stock may still work over a 12–36 month horizon, but at the current price the market is already giving STM significant credit for a recovery that still needs execution. That argues for reducing exposure rather than adding aggressively.

**Strategic Actions**: Do not initiate fresh full-size exposure in STM at current levels. For existing positions, trim into strength and reduce to below-benchmark exposure while preserving some upside participation in case the re-rating continues. Suggested implementation: take profits on 25%–40% of an existing position near current levels or on any failed move toward the $60.20 high; avoid adding unless STM either pulls back and stabilizes or delivers hard evidence of margin/free-cash-flow acceleration. Key technical levels: if STM holds $55.60–$55.70, maintain a small residual position; if it breaks that zone, consider further trimming toward $53.00–$53.60; a break below $50.00–$50.50 would materially weaken the setup and justify moving closer to minimal exposure. Rebuild only on a healthier risk/reward: either an orderly pullback toward support with improving momentum, or confirmed fundamental proof such as sustained gross-margin expansion, positive durable free cash flow, clearer LEO backlog/revenue visibility, and upward EPS revisions.
