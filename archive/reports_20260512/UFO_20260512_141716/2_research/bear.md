But investors are not buying “the space economy” in the abstract. They are buying the **Procure Space ETF**, a public-market basket with real limitations.

Many of the most exciting space companies remain private. Public holdings may include companies with:

- Heavy capital expenditure needs
- Long development cycles
- Government contract dependency
- Regulatory complexity
- Funding sensitivity
- Uncertain profitability
- Technology obsolescence risk
- Cyclical aerospace or communications exposure

The ETF structure reduces single-name risk, but it does not eliminate thematic risk. It packages it.

Thematic purity is only an advantage when the theme is attracting flows. When sentiment cools, purity becomes concentration risk.
