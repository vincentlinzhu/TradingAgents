- on-premises environments,

- Regulated sectors tend to have long sales cycles but potentially sticky contracts.
- On-premises and self-hosted capabilities can appeal to defense, government, healthcare, finance, and critical infrastructure customers.
- PATH’s legacy in automation gives it a plausible route to monetize AI through workflow orchestration rather than simply competing as another generic AI software vendor.

**Trading implication:**  
The announcement is **fundamentally positive**, but likely not an immediate revenue catalyst. The market may want proof through:
- customer wins,
- revenue acceleration,
- large contract disclosures,
- ARR growth,
- or margin expansion.

Until then, the news supports a **HOLD**, not necessarily a high-conviction BUY.
