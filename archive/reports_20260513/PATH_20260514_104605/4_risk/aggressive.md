The forward P/E near 10.5x and EV/sales around 2.2x look cheap only if the market trusts the forward earnings path. The chart says it does not.

And the agentic AI story? Strategically interesting, yes. Investable proof, no. Government and regulated industries could be attractive because of data sovereignty, compliance, auditability, and self-hosted/on-prem requirements. But those are long-cycle opportunities. The market is demanding ARR, billings, deferred revenue growth, guidance, retention, operating leverage, and SBC discipline. Until those show up, the AI narrative is optionality, not a reason to defend capital.
