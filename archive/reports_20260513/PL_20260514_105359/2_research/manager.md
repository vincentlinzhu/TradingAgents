**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: The bull side made a credible case that PL is not just a satellite-imagery vendor: revenue growth is strong at roughly 41% YoY, deferred revenue has expanded sharply, trailing operating/free cash flow have improved, liquidity is adequate, and the stock remains in a powerful technical uptrend with strategic catalysts from government demand and Google/Suncatcher optionality. I would not dismiss those points, and I would not treat PL as a broken business. But the bear side carried the debate on risk/reward at the current price. PL is trading around 47x trailing sales despite negative operating income, gross margin compression from roughly 62% to 54%, widening recent operating losses, heavy dilution, sharply higher debt, and very thin FCF yield relative to market cap. The bull thesis may ultimately be right, but too much of that future platform success is already being capitalized today. With PL near resistance around $42.20–$43.12 and highly extended versus the 50-day and 200-day averages, the stronger plan is to reduce exposure rather than chase momentum.

**Strategic Actions**: Move PL to an underweight position. For existing holdings, trim 25%–50% into current strength, especially on any failed move near $42.20–$43.12. Do not add new capital at current levels. If PL breaks and holds above $43.12 on strong volume, retain only a reduced core position rather than chase aggressively, because valuation risk remains high. If PL loses $37.50, trim further; if it breaks below the $35.20–$35.80 support zone, reduce toward a minimal tracking position. Revisit a more constructive stance only if PL either pulls back materially toward the 50-day area near $33 while fundamentals remain intact, or proves the bull thesis through sustained margin stabilization, lower dilution, larger government contracts, and consistent positive free cash flow.
