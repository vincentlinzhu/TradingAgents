## Valuation Analysis

`VOO` has a trailing twelve-month P/E ratio of approximately **29.46**. For a broad-market ETF, this reflects the weighted valuation of the underlying S&P 500 constituents.

A P/E near 29 suggests that investors are paying a relatively high multiple for current earnings. This does not automatically imply overvaluation, especially if earnings growth remains strong, but it does reduce the margin of safety.

### Price-to-Book Ratio

The reported price-to-book ratio is **1.75**. For an ETF, this metric is less directly comparable to a single operating company, but it provides a broad indication of market price relative to book value of the underlying holdings.

The reported book value is **390.85**, while the fund has traded materially above that level, indicating investors are assigning a premium to the earnings power, intangible value, and future growth prospects of the S&P 500 constituents.

### Dividend Yield

`VOO` has a dividend yield of approximately **1.08%**. This is modest and suggests that the ETF is more growth- and capital-appreciation-oriented than income-focused.

For income-seeking investors, the yield may be insufficient on its own. For long-term total-return investors, the low yield may be acceptable if earnings growth and index appreciation continue to support price gains.
