**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: The bull side made a strong case that DRAM is tied to a real AI infrastructure bottleneck: high-bandwidth memory and broader memory capacity are increasingly central to AI data-center buildouts, and DRAM has clearly become the market’s preferred ETF vehicle for that theme. The fund-flow data is genuinely impressive, with very large inflows and rapid AUM growth, and the technical trend is not broken: price remains near the recent high, above the 10 EMA/VWMA area, with MACD still positive. That said, the bear side carried the risk-adjusted argument. DRAM has nearly doubled in roughly six weeks, is still overbought, sits far above its 50-day average, has elevated ATR, cooling volume near resistance, flattening momentum, and a rich trailing valuation around 43x for a cyclical memory-linked basket. The biggest concern is that the bull case increasingly depends on continued flows and sentiment rather than margin of safety. The AI memory theme is real, but at $53–$54 the setup looks crowded and fragile. This is not a clean Sell because the trend has not broken and a breakout above $56.38 could extend momentum, but the balance of evidence favors reducing exposure rather than adding. Recommendation: Underweight.

**Strategic Actions**: Do not initiate new DRAM exposure at current levels. For existing positions, trim into strength and reduce DRAM to a below-benchmark or smaller tactical allocation, especially if the position has grown materially from the recent rally.
