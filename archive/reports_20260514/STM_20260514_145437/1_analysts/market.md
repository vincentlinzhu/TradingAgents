## Trading Plan

### For Existing Long Positions

**Recommended action:** Hold core position, but tighten risk.

Practical approaches:

- Consider taking partial profits into strength, especially if STM pushes toward **$65–$70** without consolidation.
- Trail stops using the **$60–$61 area** as a key tactical level.
- A more flexible ATR-based trailing stop could sit around **$59–$60**, depending on risk tolerance.
- If STM closes below the 10-day EMA, currently near **$57.40**, momentum traders should reassess aggressively.

The stock is still acting well, so an outright sell is premature. But the risk of a pullback is elevated enough that passive holding without a risk plan is not ideal.

### For New Long Entries

Current price is not an attractive fresh entry.

Better entry setups would include:

1. **Pullback Entry:**  
   Watch for a controlled pullback toward **$60–$61** or the 10-day EMA near **$57–$58**, followed by stabilization and renewed buying volume.

2. **Consolidation Breakout:**  
   If STM trades sideways for several sessions and holds above $60, a breakout above the consolidation high could offer a cleaner entry.

3. **Momentum Breakout Entry:**  
   Only aggressive traders should consider buying a decisive break above **$65.31**, and only if volume expands. Even then, risk should be tightly managed because the stock is already overextended.

### For Short Sellers

Shorting STM solely because it is overbought is dangerous. Strong trends can remain overbought far longer than expected.

A bearish setup would require more evidence, such as:

- Failed breakout above $65.31.
- Heavy-volume reversal candle.
- Close below $60.
- MACD histogram turning negative.
- RSI losing the 70 zone and failing to recover.

Until then, the dominant trend remains bullish.
