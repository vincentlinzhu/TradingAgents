2. **IPO strength signals improved risk appetite.**

AI momentum is a bullish tailwind, but traders should monitor whether market breadth confirms the rally. If only a narrow group of AI-related stocks is driving index highs, `VOO` becomes more vulnerable to a sharp rotation or valuation compression.
