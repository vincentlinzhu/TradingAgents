   The bullishness appears heavily tied to SpaceX speculation and media excitement. If the IPO timeline slips, sentiment may weaken.

   Space-related equities can be volatile, especially if holdings include smaller-cap aerospace, defense, satellite, or launch-related companies.

   Rapid inflows can signal strong demand, but they can also indicate crowded near-term positioning.

   Since `NASA` appears to be an ETF rather than a single operating company, investors need to analyze its holdings, expense ratio, liquidity, and factor exposures rather than treating it like a pure SpaceX proxy.

5. **Macro sensitivity**  
   High-growth thematic ETFs can be sensitive to interest rates, risk appetite, liquidity conditions, and valuation compression.
