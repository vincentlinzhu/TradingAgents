## Revenue Growth

Revenue grew from **$3.088B in Q1 2025** to **$3.770B in Q1 2026**.

That represents approximate year-over-year growth of:

**$3.770B / $3.088B - 1 = 22.1%**

This is a strong growth rate for a company already generating nearly $14B in TTM revenue.

Sequentially, revenue increased from **$3.568B in Q4 2025** to **$3.770B in Q1 2026**, or about **5.7% quarter over quarter**.

## Profitability Trends

### Gross Profit

Gross profit increased from **$2.437B in Q1 2025** to **$2.830B in Q1 2026**, up approximately **16.1% year over year**.

However, gross margin declined:

- Q1 2026 gross margin: **75.1%**
- Q1 2025 gross margin: **78.9%**

The decline suggests that cost of revenue grew faster than revenue. Cost of revenue increased from **$651M** in Q1 2025 to **$940M** in Q1 2026, a rise of approximately **44.4%**.

This is worth monitoring. It may reflect infrastructure investment, cloud delivery costs, acquisition effects, AI-related spending, or service mix changes.

### Operating Income

Operating income increased from **$451M in Q1 2025** to **$503M in Q1 2026**, up approximately **11.5% year over year**.

Operating margin declined modestly:

- Q1 2026 operating margin: **13.3%**
- Q1 2025 operating margin: **14.6%**

This indicates that while the business is scaling, expense growth continues to absorb part of the revenue upside.

### Net Income

Net income increased only slightly year over year:

- Q1 2025 net income: **$460M**
- Q1 2026 net income: **$469M**

That is growth of roughly **2.0%** year over year.

Net margin also compressed:

- Q1 2026 net margin: **12.4%**
- Q1 2025 net margin: **14.9%**

The slower net income growth versus revenue growth is one of the main caution points in the fundamental picture.

### EBITDA

EBITDA improved from **$721M in Q1 2025** to **$937M in Q1 2026**, up approximately **30.0% year over year**.

EBITDA margin improved:

- Q1 2026 EBITDA margin: **24.9%**
- Q1 2025 EBITDA margin: **23.3%**

This is a positive signal. While GAAP net income growth was muted, EBITDA growth remained strong.

- **Goodwill and intangible assets:** $6.020B

## Liquidity

The reported current ratio is **0.845**, which is below 1.0. At first glance, this could look like a liquidity concern.

However, for a subscription software company, current liabilities include large amounts of deferred revenue. Deferred revenue represents cash collected or billings for services not yet recognized as revenue. It is an accounting liability, but it is not the same as financial debt.

Current deferred revenue was **$8.030B**, making up the majority of current liabilities.

If traders adjust current liabilities by excluding current deferred revenue:

- Current liabilities: $9.983B
- Less current deferred revenue: $8.030B
- Adjusted current liabilities: approximately $1.953B
- Current assets: $8.435B

Adjusted current ratio would be approximately:

**$8.435B / $1.953B = 4.32x**

This indicates that liquidity is much stronger than the headline current ratio suggests.

## Debt and Net Cash Position

Total debt was **$2.431B**, while cash plus short-term investments were **$5.182B**.

Approximate net cash position:

**$5.182B - $2.431B = $2.751B**

This is a strong balance sheet position.

Debt-to-equity is reported at **20.728%**, which is manageable, especially given the company’s strong free cash flow generation.

## Deferred Revenue

Current deferred revenue increased from **$6.737B in Q1 2025** to **$8.030B in Q1 2026**, up approximately **19.2% year over year**.

That is a healthy signal for forward revenue visibility. Deferred revenue is particularly important for subscription software companies because it often reflects billings and contracted demand.

## Share Repurchases and Equity

Treasury stock increased substantially:

- Q1 2025 treasury stock: $1.513B
- Q1 2026 treasury stock: $5.375B

The company repurchased **$2.225B** of capital stock in Q1 2026 alone, according to the cash flow statement.

This is a major capital allocation signal. Management is actively returning capital to shareholders, but the size of repurchases also contributed to the decline in cash and equity during the quarter.

## Goodwill and Intangibles

Goodwill and other intangible assets rose to **$6.020B** in Q1 2026, up from **$4.699B** in Q4 2025 and **$1.535B** in Q1 2025.

This increase appears connected to acquisition activity. The cash flow statement shows **$1.325B** of business purchases in Q1 2026.

Traders should watch whether acquisitions contribute meaningfully to revenue growth and whether integration costs pressure margins.
