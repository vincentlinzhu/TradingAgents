**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: The bull side made the stronger strategic case, but the bear side raised enough near-term risk to keep this below a full Buy. ServiceNow `NOW` is still a high-quality enterprise software compounder: revenue and subscription growth are running around 22%, deferred revenue growth remains solid, free cash flow is substantial, the balance sheet is net cash, and the company is plausibly positioned as a workflow/governance layer for enterprise AI rather than just another AI narrative stock. The valuation reset also matters: a roughly 5% FCF yield and forward P/E below 20x are attractive if execution holds. That said, the bear case is not trivial. The chart is still damaged below the 50-day and far below the 200-day, the $100–$104.50 zone remains important resistance, GAAP margins compressed, net income growth lagged revenue badly, SBC is meaningful, and AI monetization still needs proof in bookings, cRPO, attach rates, retention, and margin leverage. Net-net, I would not call `NOW` a table-pounding Buy yet, but I also would not sit at Hold given the quality of the business, cash generation, and improved valuation after the selloff. The right call is constructive but disciplined: Overweight, implemented through staged accumulation rather than an aggressive full-position buy.
