## Bear Case and Key Risks

Despite the positive outlook, several risks are becoming more important after the stock’s rally.

### 1. Valuation Risk  
The biggest immediate risk is valuation. A stock that has risen more than 30% in 30 days and more than 70% year-to-date may already discount a large amount of good news. Even great companies can underperform if expectations become too aggressive.

### 2. Momentum Reversal  
Highly crowded trades can reverse quickly. If investors begin taking profits in AI infrastructure winners, PWR could see a sharp technical pullback even without company-specific bad news.

### 3. Execution Risk  
Large infrastructure projects are complex. Labor availability, project timing, permitting, materials costs, and subcontractor coordination can affect margins and cash flow.

### 4. Margin Pressure  
Inflation, wage growth, supply-chain constraints, or unfavorable contract mix could pressure margins. Investors are currently rewarding PWR for growth; any margin disappointment could be punished.

### 5. Interest Rate and Utility Capex Risk  
Higher financing costs can affect utility capex plans and infrastructure project economics. While grid investment is structurally necessary, timing can still shift.

### 6. AI Infrastructure Narrative Risk  
If market enthusiasm around AI data-center spending cools, PWR may be impacted by multiple compression even if its core business remains healthy.

### For Long-Term Investors  
PWR remains a high-quality infrastructure compounder with major secular tailwinds. Long-term investors can justify holding through volatility, but should be realistic about forward returns after the stock’s large advance.
