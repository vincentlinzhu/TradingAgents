## Trading Plan

### For existing long positions

**Action: HOLD.**

The trend remains bullish, and there is not enough technical evidence to justify a full exit. However, QQQ is extended and overbought, so risk should be actively managed.

Possible tactics:

- Hold while price remains above **699–700**.
- Consider trimming partial profits if QQQ fails again near **715–722**.
- Use a trailing stop below the 10 EMA or below **688–690** depending on risk tolerance.
- Watch for a MACD bearish crossover or multiple closes below VWMA as warning signals.

### For new long entries

Avoid chasing immediately after such a steep advance.

Better entry setups:

1. **Pullback entry:**  
   Look for a controlled pullback toward **699–700** or **688–690**, followed by stabilization.

2. **Breakout entry:**  
   Consider new exposure only if QQQ closes decisively above **722** with strong volume and no immediate reversal.

3. **Avoid entry if:**  
   QQQ breaks below **688–690** on heavy volume, especially if MACD turns down and RSI falls sharply.

### For short sellers

Shorting QQQ here is still risky because the trend is strongly bullish. A tactical short setup would require stronger evidence, such as:

- Failed breakout near **722**
- Close below **699–700**
- MACD rolling over toward a bearish crossover
- High-volume distribution days

Without those confirmations, bearish trades are countertrend.

## Final Recommendation

QQQ is technically strong, but short-term risk/reward is no longer favorable for fresh aggressive buying. The ETF remains above all major trend indicators, momentum is still bullish, and volume-weighted trend confirmation remains positive. However, RSI above 80 and the May 15 pullback suggest the rally may need to consolidate.

**Final transaction proposal: HOLD.**

Existing longs can stay with the trend, but should tighten risk controls. New buyers should wait for either a pullback toward support or a confirmed breakout above **722**.
