**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: Both sides agree QQQ is a high-quality long-term growth vehicle and that the primary trend is still intact. The bull case is strongest on structural leadership: QQQ remains one of the cleanest, most liquid ways to own AI, semiconductors, cloud, software, digital advertising, and mega-cap platform winners. Technically, QQQ is still above the 10 EMA, VWMA, 50 SMA, and 200 SMA, and MACD remains positive, so this is not a broken chart. But the bear side carried the debate on forward risk/reward. QQQ is near the 722.03 high after closing at 708.93, RSI is deeply stretched around 80.65, valuation is elevated at a 34.58 trailing P/E, the dividend yield offers little cushion, and the May 15 high-volume down day near resistance is a real warning. Add in sticky inflation, rising yields, oil-driven inflation pressure, and concentration in AI/semi mega-cap leadership, and the setup looks tactically vulnerable even if the long-term thesis remains attractive. This is not a Sell because QQQ leadership and trend support are still intact, but it is also not a good place to add aggressively. The stronger conclusion is to reduce risk and wait for either a healthier reset or confirmed breakout.

**Strategic Actions**: Move QQQ to a modest underweight rather than exiting. For existing positions, trim oversized exposure by roughly 20–30% and avoid adding fresh capital at current levels near 708.93. Keep a core position only if the portfolio mandate requires growth exposure, but tighten risk controls. Treat a close below 699–700 as a first warning and trim another 10–15% if it occurs. Treat a decisive break below 688–690, especially with heavy volume or MACD rollover, as confirmation to reduce QQQ further toward a minimal tactical allocation or hedge the remaining exposure. For hedging, use short-dated protective puts or collars if the trader must maintain core QQQ exposure. Rebuild exposure only under one of two conditions: either QQQ pulls back, RSI cools, and buyers defend 688–690, or QQQ closes above 722 with strong volume and holds the breakout. Until one of those conditions is met, prioritize capital preservation over chasing QQQ momentum.
