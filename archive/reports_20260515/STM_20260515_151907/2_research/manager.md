**Recommendation**: Hold

**Rationale**: Both sides agree STM is a credible semiconductor recovery story, not a broken business. The bull case is strongest on the medium-term fundamentals: revenue is up about 23% year over year, gross margin has recovered meaningfully from the trough, operating cash flow remains positive, the balance sheet is strong, and STM has attractive exposure to auto electrification, power semis, sensors, embedded systems, edge AI, IoT, and industrial markets. The bull is also right that cyclical semiconductor stocks often move before reported earnings fully normalize. However, the bear case wins on current risk/reward. STM has already rallied roughly 100%+ in less than two months and is technically stretched, with RSI near 80 and price far above the 50-day and 200-day averages. Meanwhile, the actual financial recovery is still uneven: Q1 revenue declined sequentially, gross margin slipped versus Q4, operating margin is only about 3.1%, TTM free cash flow is negative, and the forward valuation depends on a very large EPS rebound from depressed trailing earnings. That does not make STM a Sell, because the trend and long-term recovery potential are real, but it does argue against chasing or adding aggressively here. The stronger conclusion is to respect the recovery story while managing the rerating risk.

**Strategic Actions**: Maintain existing STM exposure but do not add fresh capital at current levels. For portfolios with outsized gains after the recent rally, trim 15%–25% of the position to lock in profits while retaining core exposure to the recovery thesis. Use the $58.70–$56.56 zone as the key short-term risk-control area; a decisive close below $56.50 on heavy volume should trigger a further reduction toward an underweight position. Reconsider adding only if STM either consolidates constructively and holds support, pulls back toward a more favorable risk/reward zone, or reports clear evidence of margin expansion, sustained positive free cash flow, and improved sequential demand. A breakout above $65.31 can be traded tactically only with tight sizing, but should not trigger a full fundamental add unless earnings quality also improves.
