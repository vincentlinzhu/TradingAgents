## Macro Backdrop: Important for UFO

### 1. Rising yields are the main near-term headwind  
Global market news over the past week showed that equity markets came under pressure as yields rose. Investor’s Business Daily reported that the Dow lost 500 points as yields and oil rallied.

**Why this matters for UFO:**  
UFO is exposed to a thematic growth sector. Space companies often trade on long-term revenue potential, government contracts, satellite demand, launch economics, and capital-intensive expansion. Higher discount rates reduce the present value of future cash flows and typically compress valuation multiples for speculative growth themes.

**Impact:** Bearish near term.

---

### 2. Oil rally adds inflation and cost concerns  
Oil prices rising alongside yields can create a difficult backdrop for equities. Higher oil supports inflation concerns and may make central banks more cautious about easing.

**Why this matters for UFO:**  
- Higher inflation expectations can keep yields elevated.
- Launch and aerospace-adjacent operations may face higher input and logistics costs.
- Risk appetite usually declines when oil and yields rise together.

**Impact:** Moderately bearish.

---

### 3. IPO market activity is heating up  
The past week included several IPO-related headlines, especially around Cerebras Systems. Cerebras completed its IPO, but follow-up coverage noted that the stock slid after its initial surge and remained expensive.

**Why this matters for UFO:**  
The IPO market is open, but investors are still price-sensitive. That matters because space-related companies are often valued on future growth rather than current profitability. If investors punish expensive IPOs, enthusiasm for speculative space equities may be more selective.

**Impact:** Mixed.

   Even if UFO cannot immediately hold SpaceX, thematic investors may buy UFO as a liquid proxy for the space economy.
