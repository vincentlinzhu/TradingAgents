On AI concentration, I reject the idea that this should mainly be viewed as a competitive advantage. Yes, market-cap weighting has helped VOO capture mega-cap AI leadership. But concentration is both an upside engine and a drawdown amplifier. VOO may hold hundreds of companies, but its marginal return driver is increasingly tied to a narrower group of mega-cap growth names. If that leadership weakens, VOO can fall even if many underlying constituents remain stable. For a conservative risk process, that is a reason to avoid adding aggressively after a sharp AI-led rally.

But ATR is around $6.90, which means price can approach or breach $668 quickly in normal volatility. A macro shock could gap below it. A brief break could also create whipsaw risk. So $668 should not be used as an excuse to hold with excessive confidence. It should be paired with allocation discipline, rebalancing limits, and, where needed, hedges or diversifying exposures.

A breakout above $689 could work, but under a conservative mandate, buying a breakout after an 18% rally and RSI near 79 should require a very high bar.
