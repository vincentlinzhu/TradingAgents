**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: Both sides agree on the core fundamental backdrop: DRAM has real exposure to a powerful AI memory theme, with HBM scarcity, hyperscaler AI capex, and dominant suppliers like Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron supporting the long-term story. The bull case is strongest on the structural point that AI infrastructure is broadening beyond GPUs and memory is a genuine bottleneck; if Nvidia reinforces that HBM remains constrained, DRAM could quickly regain momentum. That said, the bear side carried the debate on timing and risk/reward. DRAM has reportedly risen 85%–96% in roughly six weeks, gathered extreme flows, trades around 39x trailing earnings, and is highly concentrated with about 73% in three companies. The technical setup has also weakened: the May 18 session showed a failed rally, DRAM closed below the 10-day EMA and VWMA, momentum indicators are fading, volatility is expanding, and the 48 area is acting more like a critical trapdoor than a clean base. The theme is real, but the current entry looks crowded and catalyst-sensitive, with Nvidia expectations already high. I would not call for a full exit because the medium-term AI memory thesis remains intact and support has not decisively broken, but the evidence supports reducing exposure rather than adding here.

**Strategic Actions**: Trim DRAM exposure into strength, especially on bounces toward 51–53, rather than adding at current levels. For existing positions, reduce to a smaller tactical allocation consistent with a high-beta thematic holding, roughly below benchmark/target weight or no more than a modest satellite position. Do not initiate new exposure unless DRAM stabilizes and reclaims 51–53 with improving volume and momentum. Treat a decisive close below 48 as a risk-reduction trigger; if that occurs, cut further and watch downside zones near 46, then 42–43, and potentially 40. Ahead of Nvidia earnings, avoid increasing exposure and consider hedging or tightening stops because the catalyst is two-sided and expectations are elevated. Reassess for a more constructive stance only if DRAM holds 48, reclaims 53, and especially if it breaks above 56.38 on clear fundamental validation and sustained inflows.
