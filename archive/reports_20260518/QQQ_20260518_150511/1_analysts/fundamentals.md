## Fundamental Risks

### 1. Elevated Valuation

A **TTM P/E of 34.43** indicates a premium valuation. If earnings growth slows or market expectations reset lower, `QQQ` could face valuation compression.

This is especially important because high-duration growth assets tend to be sensitive to interest rates.

### 2. Sector Concentration

Although `QQQ` is diversified across 100 companies, it is not broadly diversified across all economic sectors. It tends to be heavily exposed to technology and growth-oriented companies.

If leadership rotates away from technology, `QQQ` may underperform broader benchmarks.

### 3. Low Income Support

The **0.42% dividend yield** provides little downside cushion. Investors are largely dependent on continued price appreciation.

### 4. Macro Sensitivity

`QQQ` is sensitive to:

- Federal Reserve policy expectations  
- Treasury yield movements  
- Inflation surprises  
- U.S. dollar strength  
- Global technology demand  
- Semiconductor cycle dynamics  
- AI-related capital expenditure trends  

### 5. Drawdown Risk

The 52-week range from **505.58 to 722.03** shows that `QQQ` can experience substantial volatility. Traders should size positions accordingly.
