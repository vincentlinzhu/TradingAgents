# QQQ Technical Trading Report — 2026-05-18

## Executive view

QQQ remains in a powerful intermediate- and long-term uptrend, but the near-term setup is extended and showing early signs of momentum fatigue. For existing long positions, the evidence supports holding, but with tighter risk controls. For new entries, chasing here is not attractive unless QQQ reclaims the recent highs with confirmation.

**Base case:** bullish trend intact, but risk/reward is less favorable after a steep advance.  
**Actionable stance:** **Hold existing longs; wait for pullback or breakout confirmation before adding.**

## Trading interpretation

### For existing long positions

**Hold**, but tighten risk management.

The trend is too strong to justify an outright sell signal, but the short-term extension and overbought RSI argue against complacency. Existing longs can remain in place while QQQ is above the $699–$701 support zone.

Do not chase aggressively at current levels.

Better long-entry setups would be:

2. **Breakout entry:** QQQ closes above $722 with strong volume and MACD confirmation.

A close below $699 would shift short-term bias from bullish to corrective and could open the door to $691, then $675–$680.
