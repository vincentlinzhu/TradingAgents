The trend remains bullish, so there is no strong technical reason to exit completely. However, because STM is extended and momentum is cooling, it would be reasonable to:

- Consider taking partial profits near **$64–$65** if price stalls.
- Use a trailing stop below **$59** for aggressive risk control.
- Use a wider stop below **$57** for swing traders willing to tolerate volatility.

### For New Buyers

This is not an ideal fresh entry point. STM is still bullish, but the stock is extended versus its moving averages and RSI remains elevated.

Better entry scenarios would be:

1. **Pullback entry:**  
   Watch for a controlled pullback toward **$57–$59** followed by bullish reversal price action.

2. **Breakout entry:**  
   Consider entry only if STM closes above **$65.31** on strong volume, ideally with RSI not making a negative divergence.

3. **Avoid chasing:**  
   Buying in the low-$60s without confirmation carries the risk of entering during a short-term consolidation or correction.
