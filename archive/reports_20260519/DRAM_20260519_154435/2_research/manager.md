**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: Both sides agree on the central fact: DRAM is tied to a real AI infrastructure bottleneck, with HBM demand strong and major memory suppliers positioned to benefit. The bull case is compelling on fundamentals: AI memory scarcity could persist into 2027, DRAM offers unusually clean exposure to Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron and related beneficiaries, and the ETF has seen very strong demand. Technically, the chart has not fully broken: price is near the 10-day EMA, MACD remains above signal, RSI has cooled from extreme levels, and buyers defended the 46.43 intraday low.

That said, the bear side carried the risk/reward argument for the current entry. DRAM has already nearly doubled from 27.76 to a 55.08 close in just over a month, then pulled back only modestly to 49.77. AUM and inflows have exploded, retail participation is high, the ETF is highly concentrated with roughly 73% in three companies, and trailing valuation near 39.6x leaves little margin for disappointment in a cyclical industry. The technical picture is not broken, but it is fragile: DRAM failed to reclaim 51–52, sits around the 10-day EMA/VWMA pivot, MACD is rolling over, and one normal ATR move could retest the 46.43–46.63 breakdown zone. Nvidia earnings may validate the theme, but with expectations so elevated, the catalyst risk is two-sided and arguably skewed toward sell-the-news if results are merely good. Net-net, the AI memory thesis remains attractive, but the current setup is too crowded and extended to recommend adding aggressively. The right stance is cautious rather than outright bearish because the fundamental theme is still intact, but the stronger argument is to reduce exposure and wait for either a deeper reset or a confirmed breakout.

**Strategic Actions**: Trim DRAM exposure rather than add at current levels. For existing positions, reduce to a below-benchmark or small satellite weight, especially on rallies into 53.70–56.38 unless DRAM breaks out on convincing volume and supportive post-catalyst fundamentals.
