## Actionable trading plan

### For existing long positions

**Maintain HOLD**, but tighten risk controls.

A reasonable tactical approach:

- Continue holding while MU remains above the **$650–$684** support zone.
- Consider reducing exposure if MU closes below **$650**.
- A more aggressive trailing stop could be placed below the 2026-05-19 low near **$652**, adjusted for position size and risk tolerance.
- A looser trend-following stop could use a 1.5–2.0 ATR band below recent prices, but that implies very large dollar risk.

Given ATR near **$49**, position sizing matters more than usual.

### For new long entries

Avoid chasing unless MU confirms strength.

Potential long triggers:

1. **Momentum reclaim setup**
   - Buy only if MU reclaims **$725–$747** with strong volume and holds above that zone.
   - This would suggest the pullback is resolving higher.

2. **Support stabilization setup**
   - Watch for a constructive base above **$650–$684**.
   - A bullish reversal candle near this zone, especially with RSI stabilizing above 50, would improve the risk/reward.

3. **Deeper pullback setup**
   - If MU pulls toward **$600–$640** and stabilizes, risk/reward may improve materially.
   - This would be preferable to buying into a volatile rebound after a parabolic move.

### For short sellers

Shorting MU remains dangerous because the primary trend is still strongly bullish. A bearish trade would require confirmation, preferably:

- Close below **$650**
- RSI weakening toward or below **50**
- MACD histogram turning negative
- Failure to reclaim VWMA/10 EMA

Until then, shorting is countertrend and high-risk.
