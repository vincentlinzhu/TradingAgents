### 2. SpaceX IPO speculation is the dominant catalyst
Barron’s reported that SpaceX may have a major week ahead, with expectations around a potential IPO update. The article cites a CNBC report suggesting that SpaceX’s S-1 registration statement could become public as soon as next week.

**Trading implication:**  
SpaceX is the flagship private company in the commercial space economy. Any concrete IPO filing would likely increase investor interest across space-related equities and ETFs, including `NASA`.

**Important caution:**  
A SpaceX IPO does not automatically mean `NASA` will immediately hold SpaceX. Investors need to monitor:

- IPO timing and valuation  
- Whether `NASA` can buy SpaceX at listing  
- Position sizing limits  
- Liquidity constraints  
- Whether SpaceX inclusion is already priced into space ETFs
