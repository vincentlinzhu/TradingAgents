## Actionable Trading Plan

### For Existing Long Positions

**Recommendation: HOLD, but tighten risk.**

QQQ remains in a strong trend, so selling aggressively is premature. However, the short-term loss of momentum argues for more disciplined trade management.

Suggested approach:

- Continue holding while QQQ remains above **$694-$695**.
- If QQQ breaks below **$694-$695**, reduce exposure or tighten stops.
- A more conservative swing stop can be placed around **$683-$685**.
- Consider partial profit-taking if QQQ fails again near **$708-$711**.
- Add only if QQQ reclaims the short-term trend and pushes back above **$711**, ideally with MACD spread widening again.

### For New Long Entries

**Do not chase here.**

Better entry setups would be:

1. **Bullish continuation entry:**  
   Buy only if QQQ reclaims **$708-$711** and then breaks toward **$719-$722** with strong volume.

2. **Pullback entry:**  
   Watch for stabilization near **$694-$695** or deeper near **$683-$685**. A bullish reversal candle near these levels would offer better risk/reward.

3. **Avoid entry if:**  
   QQQ closes below **$683-$685**, especially if MACD crosses bearish and RSI drops sharply below 60.

### For Short-Term Traders

The setup is no longer a clean momentum-long. If QQQ remains below the 10 EMA and loses **$694-$695**, a short-term mean-reversion move toward **$685** or even **$675-$682** becomes possible.

However, shorting QQQ aggressively while it remains above the 50 SMA and 200 SMA is risky. Any short trade should be tactical, not thesis-driven.
