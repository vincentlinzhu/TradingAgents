## Actionable trading plan

### For existing long positions

**Recommendation: HOLD.**

STM remains in a strong uptrend, so selling purely because the stock is overbought may be premature. However, the position should be actively managed because momentum is cooling.

Possible approach:

- Continue holding while STM stays above **$59–$60**.
- Consider trimming partial profits if STM fails again near **$63–$65**.
- Consider adding only if STM consolidates constructively or reclaims **$65.31** on strong volume.
- Use ATR-based stops because daily volatility is elevated.

### For new buyers

Avoid chasing a full position at current levels. The trend is bullish, but the risk/reward is not ideal after a major vertical move.

Better entry setups:

1. **Pullback buy:** near **$58–$60**, if price stabilizes and RSI remains above 50.
2. **Breakout buy:** above **$65.31**, but only if accompanied by strong volume and expanding momentum.
3. **Deeper reset buy:** near **$55–$56**, if STM pulls back in an orderly manner without heavy distribution.

### For short sellers

Shorting STM is risky because the dominant trend is strongly bullish. A short setup would require more confirmation, such as:

- Close below the 10-day EMA.
- MACD bearish crossover.
- RSI falling below 60 and failing to recover.
- High-volume breakdown below **$57.90–$58.00**.

Until those conditions appear, bearish trades are countertrend.

STM is technically strong, but currently extended. The stock remains above all major moving averages, volume-weighted trend support is rising, and the broader trend is bullish. **HOLD existing positions. Do not aggressively chase new longs unless STM either pulls back toward support or breaks above $65.31 with renewed momentum.**
