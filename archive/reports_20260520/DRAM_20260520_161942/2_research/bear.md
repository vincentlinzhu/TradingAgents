`DRAM` is:

- Up **85%–98%** in roughly six weeks,
- Trading around **40.97x trailing earnings**,
- About **27% above its 50-day average**,
- Highly concentrated, with about **73% in three companies**,
- Dependent on continued ETF inflows,
- Exposed to retail performance chasing,
- Vulnerable to Nvidia-related sell-the-news risk,
- Showing cooling momentum and elevated volatility.

The bull sees a powerful AI infrastructure trade with a real earnings engine.

I see that too — but I also see a trade where the price has already sprinted far ahead, the crowd is already in, and the margin for error is thin.

So my stance remains: **do not chase `DRAM` here.** Existing holders should trim, rebalance, or use strict stops around the **$49–$50**, **$48**, and **$46.43** levels. New investors should wait for a deeper pullback or clearer evidence that earnings and memory pricing can justify the rally.

The AI memory story may keep working over time. But at today’s price, investors are not being paid enough for the downside risk.
