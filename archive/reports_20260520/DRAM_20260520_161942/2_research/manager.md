**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: The bull side made a credible strategic case for DRAM: AI is increasingly a memory-bandwidth and HBM story, not just a GPU story, and DRAM offers unusually pure exposure to the dominant memory suppliers that could benefit from shortages, pricing power, and earnings upgrades into 2027. The chart also has not broken yet; DRAM remains above the 10 EMA/VWMA area around $49–$50, and a breakout above $56.38 would be meaningful. But the bear side carried the risk/reward argument for this decision. DRAM has already risen roughly 85%–98% in about six weeks, trades around 41x trailing earnings, is reportedly about 73% concentrated in Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron, and has attracted unusually large and potentially reflexive ETF flows. Momentum has cooled while volatility is elevated, and near-term upside to the $58–$59 area is not compelling enough versus downside to $45 or even the 50-day area near $40 if support breaks. So the issue is not that the AI memory thesis is wrong; it is that DRAM is now priced and positioned for a lot to keep going right. I would respect the theme but side with the bear tactically: trim risk rather than chase.

**Strategic Actions**: For existing DRAM exposure, reduce the position by roughly one-third to one-half and cap the remaining allocation at a below-target/thematic-watchlist size, especially ahead of major AI semiconductor catalysts such as Nvidia commentary. Do not initiate fresh DRAM exposure at current levels around $51–$52. Keep a small residual position only if the trader wants continued AI memory exposure, but manage it tightly: a close below $48 should trigger another trim, and a break below $46.43 should prompt reducing to a minimal tracking position or exiting tactical exposure. Reconsider rebuilding only if either: 1) DRAM pulls back toward the mid-$40s or the 50-day area near $40–$42 and stabilizes with improving flows/pricing data, or 2) DRAM closes decisively above $56.38 on strong volume after confirming catalyst news, in which case move back toward neutral before considering an overweight. Until then, prioritize capital preservation and avoid chasing DRAM’s momentum.
