## Executive View

MU remains in a powerful long-term and intermediate-term uptrend, but the stock has entered a high-volatility, late-stage momentum phase after a very large advance. **Base case:** Hold existing positions, but avoid aggressively chasing new exposure unless MU either breaks back above the recent resistance zone with strong volume or pulls back toward support in a controlled manner.
