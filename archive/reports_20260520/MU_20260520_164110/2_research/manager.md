**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: The bull side made a strong fundamental case for MU: revenue, EPS, margins, free cash flow, and the balance sheet have all inflected dramatically, and AI-driven HBM/data-center memory demand is a legitimate structural tailwind rather than a purely narrative-driven story. The forward multiple also looks optically cheap if current estimates prove even directionally right. However, the bear side carried the debate on risk/reward at the current price. MU has already moved more than 7x in a year, margins are at unusually high levels for a memory manufacturer, the forward EPS estimate requires a very large step-up from an already exceptional quarter, and free-cash-flow yield is not nearly as cheap as the P/E suggests once heavy capex is considered. Technically, MU remains in an uptrend, but it is extremely extended, volatile, and vulnerable to AI-basket/Nvidia sentiment. This is not a Sell because the company’s fundamentals and cycle momentum are too strong to abandon entirely, but it is also not a fresh Buy/Overweight setup after such a parabolic move. The strongest conclusion is to protect gains and avoid adding until either the stock resets materially or earnings durability is better proven.

**Strategic Actions**: For the trader: trim MU exposure rather than add. If currently at a full position, reduce toward a below-benchmark or half-sized position to lock in gains while preserving some upside participation in the AI memory cycle. Do not initiate new long exposure at current levels around $731.99. Use $700–$703 as the first risk-control zone; a decisive break below that should trigger additional trimming. If MU breaks below the deeper $681–$663 support area, cut exposure further or hedge aggressively, as that would confirm technical deterioration. For remaining exposure, consider protective puts or collars around major AI/Nvidia-related event risk. Revisit adding only after either a deeper pullback toward a more attractive risk/reward zone, or after MU proves that elevated margins, HBM demand, and forward earnings estimates are sustainable across multiple quarters. A confirmed breakout above $818–$819 on strong volume can justify reassessment, but only with disciplined sizing given the elevated ATR and cyclicality.
