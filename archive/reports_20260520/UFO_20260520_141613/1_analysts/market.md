## Bollinger Band and breakout context

The latest available **Bollinger Upper Band** was approximately **59.03** on 2026-05-19. UFO closed at **58.92** on 2026-05-20 and traded as high as **59.10**, meaning price is pressing directly into the upper-band area.

This is important. In weak or rangebound markets, contact with the upper Bollinger Band can signal overbought conditions. But in strong trends, price can “ride the upper band” for multiple sessions as momentum expands.

In UFO’s case, the upper-band interaction appears more like a **bullish breakout/continuation setup** than a standalone reversal warning, because:

- Price is above all major moving averages.
- RSI is strong but not above 70.
- Volume has expanded meaningfully.
- The May rally has been supported by repeated high-volume sessions.

Still, short-term traders should avoid assuming there is unlimited upside without pauses. A failure to hold above the prior breakout zone could trigger a pullback toward the 10 EMA.
