On international diversification, I think the aggressive analyst dismisses the point too easily. International equities gaining relative strength after years of lagging is not a reason to sell VOO, but it is a reason to avoid automatically allocating incremental capital to more U.S. large-cap exposure near record territory. New money can reduce portfolio volatility by being staged into international equities, bonds, cash equivalents, or other lower-correlation assets instead of adding to the same crowded leadership theme.

So my position remains clear: HOLD is appropriate, but it should be a conservative HOLD, not an upside-biased one.
