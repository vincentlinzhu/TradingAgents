## Company Profile

**MU** is Micron Technology, Inc., a major semiconductor company focused on memory and storage products. Its core markets include:

- DRAM memory
- NAND flash
- High-bandwidth memory, or HBM
- Solid-state storage
- Data center and cloud infrastructure
- AI infrastructure
- PCs, mobile devices, automotive, industrial, and embedded applications

MU is highly exposed to the semiconductor memory cycle. This means earnings can expand rapidly during periods of tight supply, rising memory prices, and strong AI/data-center demand, but can also contract sharply during oversupply periods or demand slowdowns.

The current financial data shows MU is in a powerful earnings expansion phase.
