## Actionable trading plan

### For existing long positions

**Recommendation: HOLD, but protect gains.**

A reasonable approach is to continue holding while MU remains above the **$700–$708** short-term support zone. If the position is highly profitable, consider partial profit-taking into strength near **$795–$819**, especially if volume does not expand.

Possible risk controls:

- Conservative trailing stop: below **$724**
- Wider trend stop: below **$681–$663**
- Volatility-based stop: 1 ATR below current price, roughly near **$712**
- Wider 1.5 ATR stop: roughly near **$687**

Because ATR is nearly $50, tight stops may be triggered easily. Traders should choose stop levels based on time horizon and position size.

### For new long entries

A fresh full-size buy at **$762.10** is not ideal because MU is extended and approaching resistance.

Better entry scenarios:

1. **Breakout entry**
   - Buy only on a decisive close above **$819**
   - Preferably with volume above recent average
   - This would confirm that buyers have absorbed the correction

2. **Pullback entry**
   - Look for a controlled pullback toward **$708–$700**
   - Ideally RSI holds above 50 and price stabilizes above VWMA/10-day EMA
   - This offers better risk/reward than chasing near $760+

3. **Failed-breakout avoidance**
   - If MU pushes toward $795–$819 but fails on weak volume, avoid chasing.
   - A lower high near resistance could trigger another test of $700.

### For short sellers

Shorting MU remains risky because the primary trend is strongly bullish. A short setup would require clearer evidence, such as:

- Failure below **$700**
- MACD continuing lower
- RSI losing 50
- Price breaking the recent **$681–$663** support zone

Until then, short setups are countertrend and high-risk.
