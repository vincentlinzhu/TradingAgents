**Rationale**: The bull side carried the debate, but only narrowly and with important risk controls. The bear made a strong tactical point: QQQ is stretched after a roughly 28% rally, trades near the 722 high, carries a rich 34.85x trailing PE, sentiment is crowded, momentum has cooled, and recent rebound volume has not been especially convincing. That argues against an aggressive, all-at-once buy. But the bear case still lacks price confirmation. QQQ remains above the 10 EMA, 50 SMA, and 200 SMA; the key 700–704 support zone is intact; MACD remains positive; RSI has cooled without a trend break; and QQQ continues to own the mega-cap AI, semiconductor, cloud, software, and platform leaders that are driving market earnings leadership. In other words, QQQ is expensive and crowded, but it is still leadership rather than a confirmed reversal. The right conclusion is constructive but disciplined: favor QQQ, add selectively, and let the 700 level define whether the thesis is weakening.

**Strategic Actions**: Move QQQ to a modest overweight, not a full aggressive Buy. For a benchmark/normal allocation, target roughly 110%–120% of normal QQQ exposure using staged entries. Add an initial small tranche only if the trader needs exposure, but prefer adding on a controlled pullback into 700–704 that holds. Add another tranche on a confirmed close above 722, ideally with expanding volume, with 730–735 as the first upside zone and potential for further extension if momentum broadens. Do not chase a weak-volume breakout. Use risk controls: reduce the tactical add if QQQ closes below 700; treat a break below 689 as a signal to cut back toward neutral; and reassess the full overweight thesis if QQQ begins moving toward the 637–640 50-day area. Existing holders should stay long but avoid letting QQQ become oversized beyond the planned overweight.
