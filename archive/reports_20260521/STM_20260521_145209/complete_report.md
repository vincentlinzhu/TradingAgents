## Growth potential: STM has multiple shots on goal

The bull thesis is not dependent on one narrow theme. STM has exposure to several large, expanding markets.

### 1. Automotive electrification and silicon carbide

EVs, hybrids, advanced driver assistance systems, battery management, inverters, onboard charging, and power conversion all increase chip content per vehicle.

Even if EV adoption is uneven quarter to quarter, the structural direction remains clear: vehicles are becoming electrified, connected, sensor-rich, and software-defined. STM is positioned directly in that content-growth trend.

Bears point to automotive cyclicality. Fair. But cyclicality does not erase long-term content growth. If global auto production merely stabilizes while semiconductor content per vehicle continues rising, STM can still grow.

### 2. Industrial automation and power efficiency
