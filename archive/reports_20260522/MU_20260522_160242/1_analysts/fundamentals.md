The latest quarterly data through **2026-02-28** shows a major step-change in profitability: quarterly revenue rose to **$23.86B**, gross margin expanded to roughly **74.4%**, operating margin reached approximately **67.6%**, and net income climbed to **$13.79B**.

**Bottom line:** MU’s fundamentals are strong enough to support existing positions, but the risk/reward for fresh aggressive buying is less favorable after the significant share-price move. Traders already long MU can continue holding, while new buyers may prefer pullbacks or confirmation that the current earnings level is sustainable.

# Company Profile

**MU**, Micron Technology, Inc., operates in the semiconductor industry with a focus on memory and storage products. The company is strategically exposed to markets such as data centers, AI infrastructure, PCs, mobile devices, automotive, industrial systems, and enterprise storage.

Micron’s business is highly cyclical because memory pricing and demand can fluctuate sharply across DRAM and NAND cycles. However, in upcycles, MU can generate substantial operating leverage because higher selling prices and utilization can flow rapidly into gross margin and earnings.

The latest financials suggest MU is benefiting from a powerful upcycle, likely supported by stronger demand, pricing, product mix, and operating leverage.
