**Rationale**: Both sides agree on the core facts: MU’s business momentum is exceptional, but the stock is technically stretched. The bull made the stronger fundamental case: revenue, margins, EPS, free cash flow, and balance sheet metrics have inflected dramatically, while AI-driven memory demand, tight advanced DRAM/HBM supply, CEO commentary about shortages potentially lasting beyond 2026, and expectations for DRAM pricing strength through 2027 all support a multi-quarter earnings upgrade cycle. The bear’s caution is important: MU is a cyclical, high-beta memory stock, price/book is elevated, sentiment is crowded, and the chart is far above key moving averages with MACD fatigue. However, the bear case is primarily a timing and entry-risk argument rather than evidence that the fundamental cycle has actually rolled over. Because MU still screens cheaply on forward earnings if even part of the new earnings base persists, and because current pricing/cash-flow evidence remains favorable, the recommendation should lean constructive—but not with a full aggressive Buy given the parabolic move and volatility risk.

For new or underweight accounts, build in tranches: add modestly on pullbacks that hold the $713–$718 support area, add more only on a confirmed breakout above $803–$819 with strong volume, and avoid deploying the full target position at once near $751.
