But the reports explicitly warn that inflows may dilute the SpaceX-related exposure investors are seeking. That means buyers may be paying a SpaceX-proxy premium for an ETF that may not provide clean, concentrated SpaceX exposure. That is not a minor risk. That is a potential mispricing. And mispricings should be monetized before the market corrects them.

The upside/downside math still argues for underweight, not merely target weight. From 38.75, upside to 40.00 is about 3%. Upside to 42.00 is about 8%. But downside to 36.50 is nearly 6%, downside to 35.00 is close to 10%, and a deeper mean reversion toward the 30.65 average area would be roughly 20% or more. So the neutral approach is effectively saying: keep meaningful exposure for a possible overshoot, even though the downside is larger, faster, and linked to multiple catalysts — overbought technicals, slowing MACD histogram, SpaceX headline disappointment, dilution concerns, and speculative-flow reversal.

That is not balanced. That is inertia.

The MACD point is especially important. Yes, MACD remains above signal at 1.6754 versus 1.2223. But the histogram has cooled from 0.5746 to 0.4531. That means the trend is still positive, but acceleration is fading. This is exactly the late-stage momentum profile where aggressive traders sell strength: price is still going up, but the engine is no longer accelerating at the same rate.

And the “regret risk” argument cuts both ways. Yes, we may regret trimming if NASA spikes to 42. But the trader’s plan keeps a residual underweight position, so we still participate. The bigger regret would be sitting at normal exposure and watching NASA gap lower if the SpaceX S-1 is delayed, the IPO details disappoint, investors scrutinize actual holdings, or the trade simply becomes a “buy the rumor, sell the news” event.

That is why underweight is the correct stance. Target weight is too complacent for a vehicle that has just rallied 54%, is trading near its 52-week high, sits more than 11% above the 10-day EMA, is above its upper volatility band, and is being driven by a crowded, media-amplified catalyst with unclear underlying exposure.
