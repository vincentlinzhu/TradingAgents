So I would not chase aggressively. But the bear case leans too heavily on waiting for proof that may only arrive after the stock has already rerated. At the current valuation, the risk/reward favors gradual accumulation rather than avoidance. The right stance is constructive but controlled: Overweight PATH, not an outright maximum-conviction Buy.
