**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: The bull case for RDW is credible on business momentum: revenue growth is strong, recent defense and space contract wins are real, Edge Autonomy improves the strategic positioning in tactical drones, liquidity is better, gross margin improved sharply in Q1, and free cash flow burn narrowed. That said, the bear side carried the debate for the current investment decision because the stock price has already moved as if the transition is well underway, while the financial proof is still early. RDW remains deeply unprofitable, operating expenses are far above gross profit, dilution has been severe, stock-based compensation is high, the balance sheet is heavily goodwill/intangible-driven, and valuation near 9x trailing sales is demanding for a negative-EBITDA aerospace/defense hardware company. The technical setup also matters: RDW has roughly doubled in less than a month and is materially extended above major moving averages. So while RDW is not a broken story and should remain on the watchlist, the current risk/reward favors reducing exposure rather than adding aggressively.

**Strategic Actions**: For RDW, trim exposure into strength rather than chase the rally. Existing holders should reduce the position toward a smaller, risk-managed core, especially if RDW trades into the $17.90–$18.50 resistance area. Avoid new full-size purchases at current levels. If maintaining exposure, size RDW as a speculative/high-volatility position and use disciplined risk controls: a break below $15.70 should trigger additional trimming, while a failure of the $14.60–$14.80 zone would argue for a much more defensive stance. Reconsider increasing RDW only after either a meaningful pullback that resets valuation/technicals or after the company delivers multiple quarters showing sustained mid-20% gross margins, narrowing operating losses, lower dilution, and clearer progress toward durable free cash flow.
