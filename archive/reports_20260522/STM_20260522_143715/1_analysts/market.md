### For New Buyers

Avoid chasing at the current level unless using a very short-term breakout strategy.

Better entry zones:

- Pullback toward **63–64** for aggressive buyers.
- Pullback toward **60–62** for more disciplined trend-following entries.
- Sideways consolidation above **60** with RSI cooling below **65** would improve the risk/reward profile.

### For Short Sellers

Shorting STM purely because it is overbought is risky. The trend remains strong, price is above all major averages, and volume has confirmed the move.

A short setup would require clearer evidence of failure, such as:

- close below **60–61**,
- MACD histogram turning negative and expanding lower,
- RSI losing the 60 area,

Until then, shorting remains countertrend.
