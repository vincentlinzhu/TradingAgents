**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: The bull made a credible case that STM is not structurally impaired: revenue is up meaningfully year over year from the trough, the balance sheet is strong, operating cash flow is positive, and STM has real exposure to power, analog, sensors, automotive electrification, industrial automation, silicon carbide, and second-layer AI infrastructure. If margins normalize and free cash flow turns positive, there is real operating leverage. That said, the bear side carried the debate on current risk/reward. STM has already rallied roughly 169% over the past year and more than doubled since late March, while current profitability remains very weak, free cash flow is still negative, Q1 revenue and gross margin softened sequentially, and the AI narrative is still more indirect than financially proven. At around $66.86, near the $68.14 52-week high, STM is technically stretched and priced for a strong recovery before that recovery has been fully delivered. This is not a broken-company call, but the setup argues against chasing and favors reducing exposure until either the stock resets or fundamentals confirm the bull thesis.

**Strategic Actions**: Trim STM to an underweight position, ideally around 50–75% of the trader’s normal target or benchmark weight. Do not initiate new full-size exposure at current extended levels near $66–68. For existing STM holdings, take partial profits into strength and tighten risk controls, using the $60–62 area as the key support zone; a decisive break below $60 should trigger an additional reduction. Only consider rebuilding STM exposure on an orderly pullback toward $60–62, preferably with improving momentum, or after clear evidence of sequential revenue growth, gross margin recovery above roughly 35%, and positive free cash flow. If STM breaks above $68.14 with strong volume, allow any remaining position to participate, but avoid adding aggressively unless earnings confirmation follows.
