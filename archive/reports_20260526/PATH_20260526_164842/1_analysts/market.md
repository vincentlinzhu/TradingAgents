# PATH Technical Trading Report — 2026-05-26

## Executive view

PATH has staged a short-term rebound from its May low, but the broader technical structure remains damaged. The latest raw OHLCV data shows PATH closing at **$11.09 on 2026-05-26**, up from the recent closing low of **$9.47 on 2026-05-13**, a rebound of roughly **17%** in less than two weeks. The move has been supported by elevated volume, with **51.2M shares traded on 2026-05-26**, one of the stronger recent volume days.

However, the stock is still well below its long-term trend benchmark: the **200-day SMA was near $12.98 as of the latest indicator feed**, leaving PATH in a longer-term downtrend despite the improving short-term setup. The stock is now testing an important resistance zone around **$11.25–$11.40**, which corresponds to the recent Bollinger upper-band area and the 2026-05-26 intraday high.

My base recommendation is therefore **HOLD**, not an outright buy yet. Existing holders can continue to hold while momentum improves, but new entries are better timed either on a confirmed breakout above **$11.40–$11.50** or on a controlled pullback toward **$10.60–$10.75** that holds as support.

The stock has also recovered above the latest **50-day SMA of $10.76 as of 2026-05-22**. The 50-day SMA had been declining from above **$12.11 in late March** to the high **$10.70s** by late May, reflecting prior medium-term weakness. But price reclaiming this area is notable because it suggests the recent rally is no longer just a dead-cat bounce below all major moving averages.

**Actionable implication:**  
As long as PATH holds above approximately **$10.60–$10.75**, the short-term recovery remains intact. A close back below that zone would weaken the bullish rebound thesis.

The 50-day SMA is still sloping downward, which means the medium-term trend has not fully turned bullish. PATH needs time above the 50-day SMA to flatten and eventually turn the average higher.

The immediate issue is that price is now moving into resistance rather than fresh support. The recent high on 2026-05-26 was **$11.40**, and the latest Bollinger upper band from the indicator feed was **$11.26 as of 2026-05-22**. That makes the **$11.25–$11.40** zone a key test area.

**Actionable implication:**  
A daily close above **$11.40–$11.50**, ideally with sustained volume, would improve the setup materially and could open room toward **$12.00–$12.45**.

---

### Long-term trend: still bearish

The major technical problem is the **200-day SMA**, which was approximately **$12.98 as of 2026-05-22**. PATH remains significantly below that level. This tells us that, despite the short-term rebound, the stock is still in a long-term downtrend.

The broader context is important:

- PATH peaked near **$19.84 intraday on 2025-12-08**.
- It then sold off heavily into February, March, April, and May.
- The April/May lows were in the **$9.20–$9.50** area.
- The current price near **$11.09** is a recovery, but not yet a trend reversal on the long-term chart.

**Actionable implication:**  
Long-term investors should be cautious until PATH can reclaim at least the **$12.00–$13.00** zone. The 200-day SMA near **$13.00** remains the larger regime-change level.

## Volatility and Bollinger Band analysis

### Price is near the upper Bollinger zone

The latest Bollinger upper band was **$11.26 as of 2026-05-22**, while PATH reached **$11.40 intraday on 2026-05-26** before closing at **$11.09**. This suggests the stock is pressing against the upper volatility boundary.

That does not automatically mean PATH should be sold. In strong reversals, prices can ride or expand the upper band. But in a stock still below its 200-day SMA, upper-band tests often become short-term resistance unless confirmed by follow-through.

The latest Bollinger lower band was around **$9.71 as of 2026-05-22**, which aligns well with the recent May breakdown/reversal zone. This makes **$9.70–$10.00** an important lower support area if the rebound fails.

**Actionable implication:**  
A breakout above **$11.40–$11.50** would be bullish. Failure near the upper band followed by a close below **$10.75** would suggest a return to range-bound or bearish conditions.

### ATR: volatility remains meaningful

ATR was approximately **$0.61 as of 2026-05-22**. With PATH trading near **$11.09**, ATR represents about **5.5% of price**, which is significant. This stock can move quickly, so position sizing should be conservative.

For traders:

- A tight stop may get shaken out easily.
- A volatility-adjusted stop should account for at least **1 ATR**.
- A more patient swing-trade stop could use **1.5 ATR**, depending on entry.

Using the latest ATR:

- 1 ATR below $11.09 ≈ **$10.48**
- 1.5 ATR below $11.09 ≈ **$10.17**

**Actionable implication:**  
For new bullish trades, a stop below **$10.45–$10.50** is reasonable for tighter risk. A wider swing stop below **$10.15–$10.20** gives the trade more room but increases downside exposure.
