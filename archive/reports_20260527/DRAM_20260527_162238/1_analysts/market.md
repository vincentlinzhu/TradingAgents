**Preferred action: Hold, but manage risk actively.**

Partial profit-taking is reasonable given the stock’s extreme move. A trader could trim some exposure into strength while keeping a core position for a breakout continuation.

### For new long entries

Fresh entries are less attractive at current levels because DRAM is extended above its 10 EMA, 50 SMA, VWMA, and near the upper Bollinger Band.

Better entry setups:

1. **Breakout entry:** Buy only if DRAM clears **$62.68** with strong volume and holds above that level.  
2. **Pullback entry:** Wait for a controlled retracement toward **$57**, **$55**, or ideally the **$52–53** 10 EMA/VWMA area.  
3. **Avoid entry:** If price breaks below **$57** on heavy selling volume, wait for stabilization.

### For short sellers

Shorting DRAM is high risk while the trend and volume remain this strong. A short setup would require clear evidence of failed breakout, heavy distribution, and a break of key support. Until then, bearish trades are countertrend.
