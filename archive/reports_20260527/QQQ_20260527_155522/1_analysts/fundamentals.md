## Valuation Analysis

### P/E Ratio

`QQQ` has a trailing P/E ratio of **35.58**, which indicates that investors are paying a premium for expected future earnings growth from the Nasdaq-100 constituents.

This valuation is not necessarily excessive for a growth-heavy ETF, but it does leave less margin of safety. A P/E in the mid-30s implies that investors expect continued earnings growth, particularly from mega-cap technology and AI-related names.

### Interpretation for Traders

- A high P/E ratio supports the view that `QQQ` is priced for strong future growth.
- If earnings growth remains robust, valuation can be sustained.
- If growth slows or interest rates remain high, `QQQ` could be vulnerable to multiple compression.
- Long-term investors may continue holding, but short-term traders should be mindful of downside risk after strong rallies.

### Price-to-Book Ratio

The price-to-book ratio is **2.04**. For an ETF like `QQQ`, this is less useful than it would be for a bank, industrial company, or asset-heavy business. Many Nasdaq-100 companies are asset-light and derive value from intellectual property, software, platforms, and network effects rather than tangible book value.

Still, the relatively moderate price-to-book ratio compared with the high P/E suggests that book value is not the key valuation anchor for this ETF. Earnings growth and forward expectations matter more.
