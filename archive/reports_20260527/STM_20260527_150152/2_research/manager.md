**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: The bull case for STM is credible on company quality: STM has a strong balance sheet, positive operating cash flow, strategic European semiconductor exposure, and real long-term optionality in power semis, automotive electrification, industrial, analog, silicon carbide, and AI-infrastructure adjacency. The market is also right to look through trough earnings to some degree, because semiconductor stocks often rerate before margins fully recover. That said, the bear side carried the debate on risk/reward at the current price. STM has already roughly doubled in about two months, while Q1 profitability remains very thin, free cash flow is still pressured by structural capex, revenue and operating income weakened sequentially, and the AI benefit is still more narrative than proven in reported earnings. The technical setup is also stretched, with RSI near 80 and a rejection around the $70–$71 zone. So this is not a broken-company call, but the price now embeds a lot of recovery before STM has delivered enough margin and free-cash-flow proof. The better plan is to protect gains and wait for either a cleaner entry or stronger fundamental confirmation.

**Strategic Actions**: For STM, trim exposure rather than add. Reduce any existing position to below benchmark or roughly 50–75% of a normal target weight, especially after the sharp rally. Do not initiate new exposure at current levels near the high-$60s unless the mandate requires semiconductor exposure. Use $70–$71 as a resistance/profit-taking zone; if STM fails there again, trim further. Treat a break below the 10 EMA area near $64 on heavy volume as a tactical risk-reduction trigger. A pullback toward the $61.90–$62 VWMA area can be reviewed, but only start rebuilding if selling volume fades and the stock stabilizes. Reconsider upgrading STM only if the company delivers clear evidence of margin recovery, sustainable positive free cash flow, and stronger sequential revenue/earnings momentum, or if the stock resets to a materially better valuation.
