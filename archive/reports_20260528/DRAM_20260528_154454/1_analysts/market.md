## Trading Plan

### For Existing Long Positions

**Recommendation: Hold, but tighten risk management.**

The trend remains bullish. Price is above the 10 EMA and 50 SMA, MACD is above its signal line, and volume supports the move. Selling solely because RSI is overbought may be premature.

However, the stock is extended. Traders should consider trailing stops or partial profit-taking.

Possible risk-management approaches:

- Aggressive trailing stop: below **$59.00**
- More flexible swing stop: below **$55.00–$56.00**
- Trend-following stop: below the 10 EMA, currently around **$54.32**
- Partial profit-taking zone: into strength above **$63.64–$66.50**

If DRAM loses $59 with volume, the late-May breakout could be failing. If it loses $55, the pullback would become more meaningful.

### For New Buyers

**Avoid chasing at current levels unless using a short-term breakout strategy.**

A fresh long at $62.57 has unfavorable risk/reward unless the trader is specifically trading momentum breakouts with tight discipline.

Better entries may occur on:

1. A pullback toward **$59–$60** that holds.
2. A deeper pullback toward **$55–$56** that stabilizes.
3. A multi-day consolidation above **$60** followed by a breakout above **$63.64** on strong volume.

A breakout entry above **$63.64** is possible, but it should be treated as a high-volatility momentum trade, not a low-risk investment entry.

### For Short Sellers

Shorting DRAM is risky while the trend remains this strong. Overbought does not mean immediate reversal. A short setup would require evidence of failure, such as:

- Rejection above $63.64,
- Close back below $60,
- MACD rolling over,
- RSI divergence,
- Heavy distribution volume.

Until then, shorting is fighting the prevailing trend.

---

## Bottom Line

DRAM is technically bullish, but extended. The strongest evidence supports **holding existing long exposure**, not aggressively adding new exposure at current levels.

The stock is in a powerful breakout trend with bullish MACD confirmation and strong volume. However, RSI remains overbought, price is near the upper Bollinger Band, and ATR has expanded sharply. That combination suggests upside momentum is real, but near-term pullback risk is elevated.

**Final stance: HOLD.**  
Existing longs can stay with the trend while using trailing stops. New buyers should wait for either a controlled pullback or a clean consolidation breakout.
