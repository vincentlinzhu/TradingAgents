This sounds scary, but it needs context.

A trailing P/E of **49.77x** reflects historical earnings, not necessarily forward earnings power. Memory is cyclical, and trailing earnings often look least attractive near the beginning of an earnings upcycle because margins have not yet fully reflected stronger pricing, tighter supply, and higher utilization.

If HBM pricing and demand continue to improve, the “E” in the P/E can rise meaningfully. In other words, today’s high trailing multiple may compress through earnings growth rather than price decline.

The bear is using a backward-looking valuation metric against a forward-looking structural demand story.

The key bull argument is not that memory cyclicality has disappeared. It is that the current cycle has a stronger structural demand driver than prior PC/smartphone-led cycles.

AI workloads are memory-intensive. As models grow larger and inference workloads scale, bandwidth and capacity become increasingly important. That supports a more durable demand curve.

So yes, supply will eventually respond. But if order books extend beyond 2027, the market may be underestimating how long the favorable part of this cycle can last.
