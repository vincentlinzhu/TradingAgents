## Trading plan

### For existing long positions

**Recommendation: HOLD, but manage risk actively.**

Suggested approach:

- Consider taking partial profits into the **$950–$1,000** zone.
- Maintain the core position while MU holds above **$888–$895**.
- A daily close below **$888** would be an early reason to reduce exposure.
- A break below **$820** would signal a more significant loss of momentum.
- A move below the 10 EMA area, currently near **$787**, would be a stronger trend warning.

### For new buyers

This is not an ideal fresh entry. MU is too extended above its moving averages, RSI is overbought, and ATR is very high.

Better entry setups:

1. **Breakout entry:**  
   Buy only if MU closes above **$956** with strong volume and holds above that level. Use smaller size due to volatility.

2. **Pullback entry:**  
   Wait for a pullback toward **$820–$790**, especially if RSI cools toward **60–65** and price stabilizes.

3. **Avoid chasing:**  
   Buying after a vertical move near the upper Bollinger Band offers poor asymmetry unless the trader is using a very short-term momentum strategy.

### For aggressive short-term traders

A tactical short is not favored while MACD remains bullish and price is above $895. However, a failed breakout near **$950–$956**, followed by a close below **$888**, could open downside toward **$820–$790**.

Short setups should be treated carefully because the primary trend remains strongly bullish.

---

## Final recommendation

MU is technically strong, but it is also heavily extended. The dominant trend is bullish across the 10 EMA, 50 SMA, and 200 SMA, and MACD has reaccelerated positively. However, RSI is overbought, price is near the upper Bollinger Band, and ATR shows unusually high volatility.

The best current stance is **HOLD** for existing positions, not an aggressive fresh buy. Traders should protect gains, consider partial profit-taking near resistance, and wait for either a confirmed breakout above **$956** or a healthier pullback toward the **$820–$790** support zone.
