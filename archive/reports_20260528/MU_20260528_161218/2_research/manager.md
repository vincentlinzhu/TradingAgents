**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: Both sides agree MU’s fundamentals are exceptional: revenue, EPS, margins, cash flow, and balance sheet strength have all improved dramatically, and the AI/HBM memory thesis is real rather than promotional. The bull side made the stronger long-term fundamental case: MU is benefiting from a genuine AI infrastructure bottleneck, HBM scarcity, large positive estimate revisions, strong free cash flow even after heavy capex, and a forward valuation that could remain reasonable if the earnings reset is durable. However, the bear side made the stronger near-term portfolio-management argument. MU has rallied roughly 862% in a year, is extremely extended versus moving averages, sits near the $949–$956 resistance/exhaustion zone, has elevated RSI and ATR, and is exposed to crowded/leveraged positioning. The key issue is not whether MU is a good company — it is — but whether the current entry offers enough margin of safety after a parabolic move. I would not sell the whole position because the fundamental thesis is too strong, but I also would not add aggressively here. The best stance is constructive but risk-controlled: maintain core exposure, avoid chasing, and add only on a reset or confirmed breakout. That supports an Overweight rather than a full Buy.

**Strategic Actions**: Maintain or modestly increase MU exposure, but do it tactically rather than chasing a full allocation at current levels. For existing holders, keep a core position but trim any oversized trading exposure into strength near $949–$956. For new capital, build in stages: add a small starter/partial tranche only if needed for exposure, add more on a decisive close above $956 with strong volume, or preferably buy a pullback toward the $820–$790 support area if fundamentals remain intact. Use $888–$895 as the near-term risk-control zone; a close below that level should trigger reducing trading shares or hedging. Position sizing should reflect high volatility: target a moderately above-benchmark allocation, not an aggressive maximum-weight position, and avoid leverage.
