## 1. Company Profile

`NOW` is ServiceNow, Inc., a large-cap enterprise software company focused on digital workflow automation. The company’s platform helps enterprises automate IT service management, customer service workflows, employee workflows, security operations, IT operations, and other business processes. ServiceNow is typically viewed by investors as a high-quality SaaS / enterprise software compounder due to recurring subscription revenue, strong gross margins, high free cash flow generation, and durable enterprise demand for workflow digitization.

The company’s fundamentals remain strong, but valuation and near-term trading context argue for a **HOLD** rather than an aggressive new-money BUY at current levels.

- **Current assets:** **$8.44B**
- **Current liabilities:** **$9.98B**
- **Working capital:** **negative $1.55B**
- **Current ratio:** **0.845**

### Liquidity

The current ratio below 1.0 and negative working capital may appear concerning at first glance. However, for a SaaS company, a large portion of current liabilities is deferred revenue.

Current deferred revenue was **$8.03B** as of Q1 2026. Deferred revenue reflects customer payments or billings before revenue recognition, so it is more of a future revenue obligation than a traditional cash liability.

Still, traders should monitor this line carefully because deferred revenue is a useful signal of enterprise demand, bookings strength, and future revenue visibility.

### Net cash position

Using cash and short-term investments of **$5.18B** against total debt of **$2.43B**, `NOW` has an approximate net cash / investment surplus of **$2.75B**.

This gives ServiceNow strategic flexibility for:

- acquisitions,
- buybacks,
- product investment,
- AI/platform expansion,
- and sales capacity expansion.

### Goodwill and acquisitions

Goodwill and intangible assets increased meaningfully:

- **$4.70B at 2025-12-31**
- to **$6.02B at 2026-03-31**

Cash flow data shows **$1.33B of business purchases** in Q1 2026. This suggests acquisition activity contributed to the increase in goodwill and intangible assets.

That is not necessarily negative, but it creates integration risk and increases the importance of monitoring whether acquisitions contribute to growth, product differentiation, and retention.

### Share repurchases and equity

Treasury stock rose materially to **$5.38B** in Q1 2026, compared with **$3.05B** at 2025-12-31. The company repurchased **$2.23B** of stock in Q1 2026.

Repurchases are supportive, but traders should also watch stock-based compensation dilution.

## 9. Recommendation: HOLD

`NOW` remains a fundamentally strong enterprise software company with excellent revenue growth, robust cash flow, and a solid balance sheet. However, the stock’s valuation still depends on continued high growth and successful margin expansion.

A **HOLD** is appropriate because the risk/reward is balanced:

- Existing investors can continue holding due to strong fundamentals and cash flow.
- New buyers may want to wait for either a more attractive entry point or confirmation of further revenue acceleration, billings strength, and margin expansion.
- Traders should be cautious about chasing unless forward estimates continue rising.

### Conditions that could justify upgrading to BUY

Consider a more aggressive bullish stance if:

- Revenue growth remains above **20%**,
- free cash flow margin remains above **35%**,
- deferred revenue / bookings trends improve,
- share repurchases reduce diluted share count despite SBC,
- acquisition integration appears accretive,
- and valuation remains near or below current forward multiples.

### Conditions that could justify SELL / reduce exposure

Consider reducing exposure if:

- revenue growth falls below the high-teens range,
- free cash flow margin compresses materially,
- SBC remains elevated without share-count improvement,
- acquisition-related goodwill continues rising without clear growth payoff,
- or forward P/E expands without corresponding earnings estimate increases.
