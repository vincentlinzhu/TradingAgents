**Avoid chasing aggressively at 735+ unless using a short-term breakout strategy.**

Better entry zones:

1. **Pullback entry:**

- price losing **715**,  
- RSI breaking down while price fails near highs,

Until then, bearish trades are countertrend and higher risk.
