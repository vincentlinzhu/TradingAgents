
Bear Analyst: ## Bear Case for QQQ: The Bull Is Calling It “Leadership,” But This Looks Like Late-Cycle Crowding

I agree with the bull on one thing: QQQ is in a strong uptrend. The chart is not broken. But that is exactly why the risk is elevated now. The bear case is not “short QQQ blindly because it went up.” The bear case is that **the easy money has already been made, the ETF is priced for perfection, and the current entry point offers poor risk/reward**.

QQQ is trading around **735.60**, essentially at its **52-week high of 736.60**, after a roughly **31% rally from the late-March low near 558.28**. That is a huge move in a short period. The bull says this is “leadership.” I’d call it **crowded momentum at a premium valuation, with macro risks rising at exactly the wrong time**.

My stance: **do not chase QQQ here. Existing holders should consider trimming or hedging, especially if overweight. New buyers should wait.**
