Target-weight accounts can hold, but any account that has drifted overweight because of the recent rally should be trimmed now toward neutral or slight-underweight. I would not wait for a perfect 740–750 exit. Markets do not always give investors clean exits, especially when the risk drivers are inflation, oil, and geopolitics.

Rotation risk also deserves more respect than the aggressive analyst gives it. Value is reportedly outperforming growth year-to-date, and Japan and Korea are attracting capital. That does not mean QQQ must fall, but it does mean the firm should avoid excessive concentration in richly valued U.S. mega-cap growth. For a stability-focused portfolio, relative underperformance and volatility concentration matter.

So the final stance remains HOLD, but the implementation should be explicitly defensive. Maintain core exposure because the trend is intact, but do not add at 735+. Trim overweight accounts now or into any additional strength. Use hedges, collars, or partial profit-taking for concentrated or leveraged exposure. Treat 715 as a tactical stop, but monitor 729–730, 722, 710–711, and 700–705 because deterioration may begin before the formal stop is reached.

The aggressive analyst is right that selling outright would be premature. The neutral analyst is right that participation still matters. But the safest path for the firm is protected participation, not confident participation. QQQ remains a HOLD, but only as a risk-managed, defensive HOLD designed to preserve gains and avoid avoidable downside.
