## Valuation Analysis

### P/E Ratio

`VOO` has a trailing P/E ratio of approximately **28.38**. For a broad-market U.S. equity ETF, this is elevated relative to long-run historical market averages. A high P/E ratio can be justified if earnings growth remains strong, interest rates are supportive, and investor risk appetite remains high. However, it also means the ETF may be more vulnerable to:

- Earnings disappointments among large-cap companies
- Higher interest rates
- Inflation surprises
- Multiple compression
- Weakening economic growth

### Price-to-Book Ratio

The reported price-to-book ratio is **1.78**. For an ETF, this metric reflects aggregate or vendor-derived underlying portfolio valuation rather than a corporate book value in the traditional sense.

A P/B near this level is not alarming for a broad equity ETF, but investors should note that book value is less informative for modern large-cap equity indices because many of the largest companies are asset-light businesses with significant intangible value.

### Dividend Yield

`VOO` has a dividend yield of **1.08%**. This is relatively modest and reinforces that `VOO` is primarily a capital appreciation vehicle rather than an income-oriented investment.

For income-focused traders or investors, the low yield may be insufficient. For long-term growth investors, the yield is acceptable, especially if earnings growth and buybacks continue to support equity returns.
