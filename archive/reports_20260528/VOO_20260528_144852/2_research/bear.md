- Inflation gradually cools  
- The Fed eventually has room to ease  
- Geopolitical shocks remain contained

## 5. Concentration Risk Is Being Underestimated

That is not inherently bad, but it reduces the diversification benefit investors think they are getting.

The bull says mega-cap companies have strong balance sheets and high-quality earnings. Fine. But high-quality assets can still be overpriced. The Nifty Fifty were high-quality. At a **28.38 trailing P/E**, that risk is real.
