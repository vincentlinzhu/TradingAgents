## Trading Plan

### For Existing Long Positions

**Preferred action: HOLD.**

The trend remains bullish, and momentum is confirmed by price, MACD, VWMA, and volume. However, the position should be actively managed because the stock is extended.

Suggested risk management:

- Conservative trailing stop: **below $59.70–$60.00**
- Wider trend stop: **below $56.00–$58.00**
- Consider partial profit-taking if price fails repeatedly near **$64.29–$65.00**
- Maintain exposure if DRAM closes above **$65.00** on strong volume

### For New Long Entries

Avoid chasing a full position at current levels unless DRAM confirms a breakout.

Potential entry approaches:

1. **Breakout entry:**  
   Buy only on a decisive close above **$65.00**, preferably with volume above **35–40 million shares**.

2. **Pullback entry:**  
   Look for support near **$60.00**, **$58.00**, or the rising 10 EMA area near **$56.00**.

3. **Failed breakout caution:**  
   If DRAM trades above **$64.29–$65.00** intraday but closes weakly below that area, short-term traders should be cautious of a momentum reversal.

### For Short Sellers

Shorting DRAM is not favored while the trend and MACD structure remain bullish. A short setup would require clearer evidence of exhaustion, such as:

- Failed breakout above **$65**
- RSI bearish divergence
- MACD rolling below its signal line
- Close below **$59–$60**
- Heavy-volume downside reversal

Until those conditions appear, shorting is counter-trend and high risk.
