**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: Both sides agree on the basic identity of IONQ: it is a real, strategically interesting quantum-computing pure play with exceptional thematic appeal, rapid year-over-year revenue growth, a very strong balance sheet, and strong recent momentum. The bull case is strongest on the point that IONQ has moved beyond tiny proof-of-concept revenue, with Q1 revenue rising from $7.6M to $64.7M year over year, and that $2.03B of cash and short-term investments gives the company time to pursue a long-duration quantum opportunity. The bull is also right that the chart is not broken: IONQ remains above key moving averages, and a breakout above roughly $66.80–$67.00 could extend momentum.

That said, the bear side carried the debate on risk/reward at the current price. IONQ is trading around 130x TTM sales and roughly 94x annualized Q1 sales, while the core business remains deeply unprofitable: Q1 operating loss was about -$271.5M on $64.7M of revenue, gross margin fell to roughly 23.8%, free cash flow was -$159.4M, SBC was very high, and share count rose about 54% year over year. The recent government-funding narrative validates quantum broadly, but IONQ was not clearly identified as a direct recipient, so the rally may be partly sympathy-driven rather than company-specific. Technically, IONQ has rallied roughly 146% from late March, RSI is above 70, ATR is high, and near-term upside to $70–$73 does not clearly compensate for downside to $58–$55 support. In short, the long-term story is compelling, but the stock already discounts a lot of success. The strongest conclusion is not to sell aggressively or short into momentum, but to reduce exposure into strength and wait for a better entry or stronger company-specific proof.

**Strategic Actions**: For IONQ, trim exposure rather than add at current levels. Existing holders should reduce the position to a below-benchmark or half-sized speculative allocation, especially into strength near or above the $66.80–$67.00 breakout zone unless volume and company-specific news confirm the move. Do not initiate a new full position at $65+ after the recent 146% rally. If the trader must maintain exposure for thematic quantum participation, keep IONQ as a small, risk-controlled position only.

Use tactical levels: consider further trimming if IONQ fails to hold above $67 after a breakout attempt, or if it loses the $60–$58 area, where the 10-day EMA sits nearby. A reset toward $55, or evidence of direct IONQ government awards, bookings acceleration, gross-margin recovery, slower cash burn, or reduced dilution/SBC pressure would be needed before upgrading the stance. Given high beta and ATR, use tight risk controls, avoid leverage, and consider hedges or collars if holding a meaningful position through macro or policy headlines.
