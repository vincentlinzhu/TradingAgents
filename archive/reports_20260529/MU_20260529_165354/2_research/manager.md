**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: Both sides made credible points on MU. The bull case is stronger on the business: revenue, margins, EPS, operating cash flow, free cash flow, and balance sheet trends are all pointing to a major AI-memory earnings inflection rather than a routine memory-cycle rebound. Dell’s AI-server momentum, tight memory supply, HBM/cloud memory mix, and rising pricing across end markets support the view that MU’s forward earnings power may still be underestimated despite the big move. The valuation also is not obviously excessive if the forward EPS ramp is even directionally right. That said, the bear side is right on timing and risk: MU is extremely extended after a roughly 79% one-month rally, sentiment is crowded, RSI and distance from moving averages are stretched, and a normal technical reset could mean 15–25% downside without breaking the long-term thesis. The biggest fundamental risks are peak-like gross margins, Samsung/SK Hynix competition, capex-driven future oversupply, and working-capital/receivables pressure. Net/net, the bull arguments win on fundamentals, but the bear arguments prevent a full Buy call at the current entry point. The right stance is constructive but disciplined: stay exposed to MU and gradually add, preferably through pullbacks or consolidation rather than chasing a full position immediately.

**Strategic Actions**: For MU, move toward a modest overweight position gradually rather than buying a full allocation at once. If underweight or uninvested, initiate only a starter position of roughly 25–35% of intended MU exposure near current levels, then add another 25–35% only if MU consolidates constructively or pulls back into the $923–$895 support zone and holds. Reserve the final tranche for either a successful retest after a clean breakout above $1,000 or a volatility reset closer to the rising short-term moving average. Existing holders should maintain a core MU position, but avoid letting the position become oversized after the parabolic rally; keep exposure capped according to portfolio risk limits. Use ATR-aware risk controls given roughly 5–6% daily volatility: treat a decisive break below $895 as a short-term warning to pause additions and reduce tactical exposure, and reassess more aggressively if margin/pricing data weakens, receivables worsen, or Samsung/SK Hynix news points to real share or pricing pressure. Do not short MU solely on overbought technicals; the fundamental trend remains too strong.
