**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: NBIS has a genuinely powerful bull case: revenue is scaling at an extraordinary pace, gross margin around 74% is unusually strong for an AI infrastructure buildout, deferred revenue near $4.78B gives real demand visibility, and the Situational Awareness stake adds meaningful institutional validation. The bull side is right that this is not just an AI-label story — customers are committing and revenue is showing up quickly. That said, the bear case lands on valuation and execution risk. NBIS is trading at roughly 67x TTM sales, remains operating-loss and free-cash-flow negative, has taken debt up sharply, and is pursuing a $20B–$25B capex program before proving durable returns on that capital. The stock is also technically extended after a major run. Net-net, I think the bull arguments carry the strategic debate, but not enough for a full Buy at this entry. NBIS deserves exposure as a potential AI infrastructure winner, but the position should be built carefully rather than chased aggressively.

**Strategic Actions**: For NBIS, maintain or build an overweight position gradually rather than buying a full allocation at once. For existing holders, keep the core position but trim only if NBIS has become oversized beyond risk limits. For new or underweight accounts, start with a partial position of roughly one-third to one-half of the intended allocation, then add on pullbacks into the $207–$221 support zone or on a decisive high-volume breakout above $233.60. Keep position sizing volatility-aware: cap NBIS around 3%–5% of a diversified growth portfolio, lower for risk-constrained mandates. Use risk controls: a close below $207–$208 would be a warning to pause adds, and a heavy-volume break below $195–$200 should trigger a review or reduction. Monitor upcoming updates for capex financing terms, dilution, utilization, gross margin durability, operating-loss improvement, and conversion of deferred revenue into recognized revenue.
