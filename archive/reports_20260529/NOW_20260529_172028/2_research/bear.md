The market is pricing in AI upside, but the reports specifically note there was no new company-specific AI monetization data this week. Investors need to see actual adoption metrics, pricing uplift, renewal strength, RPO growth, and large-deal momentum — not just narrative enthusiasm.

The “AI workflow platform” story is plausible. But plausible is not enough when the stock trades at premium multiples.

M&A is another concern. Goodwill and intangibles rose to **$6.02B**, up from **$4.70B** in Q4 2025 and **$1.54B** a year earlier. That introduces:

- Integration risk.
- Overpayment risk.
- Margin dilution.
- Product complexity.
- Future impairment risk.
- Potential dependence on acquisitions to support the AI narrative.

Bull, you call M&A a strategic lever. Fair. But it also makes the story less clean for a company being valued like a pristine organic compounder.

## 7. The AI workflow thesis is credible — but the economics are not guaranteed.

This is the biggest point of disagreement.

I agree that AI agents may need workflows, approvals, audit trails, governance, ticketing, escalation paths, and enterprise integration. That does fit ServiceNow’s domain.

But the key question is not whether the use case exists.

The key question is: **who captures the economics?**

ServiceNow is not competing in an empty field. The enterprise AI workflow layer will be contested by:

- Microsoft
- Salesforce
- Oracle
- Workday
- Cloud hyperscalers
- Security platforms
- Data platforms
- Automation vendors
- AI-native startups

Being embedded helps. It does not guarantee pricing power.

AI could also pressure ServiceNow in several ways:

- Customers may expect AI features to be bundled rather than separately monetized.
- Workflow automation may become easier to build internally.
- Microsoft and cloud platforms may absorb more workflow logic.
- AI-native tools may undercut traditional workflow modules.
- CIOs may scrutinize expensive SaaS contracts more aggressively.

So yes, ServiceNow could benefit from AI. But the stock is already starting to price in that outcome before we have hard evidence.

I want to see actual proof: AI attach rates, pricing uplift, RPO acceleration, renewal strength, large-deal momentum, and margin contribution. Right now, we have a good story, not enough numbers.
