**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: The bull made the stronger strategic case on NOW, but the bear made the stronger tactical timing case. On the positive side, NOW remains a high-quality enterprise software compounder with roughly 22% revenue growth, 75% gross margins, more than $5B of trailing free cash flow, a net cash balance sheet, large deferred revenue visibility, and a credible role as an AI-enabled workflow orchestration platform. The bear is right that the 14% one-day move was not driven by a NOW-specific catalyst, the stock is stretched near $125 resistance, still below the 200-day zone around $141–$145, and GAAP earnings leverage plus SBC/M&A deserve monitoring. But those risks argue against chasing a full position immediately, not against owning the name. The business quality, cash generation, AI workflow positioning, and high-volume technical reversal outweigh the valuation and timing concerns. I would not call this a full Buy because the near-term entry is extended, but the evidence supports a constructive stance rather than trimming core exposure.

**Strategic Actions**: For NOW, maintain or build toward an overweight position gradually rather than buying all at once after the spike. Keep existing core exposure intact unless position size is already excessive. Add modestly only on consolidation above $116–$118 or on a successful retest of the $108–$110 breakout zone. If NOW clears and holds above $125, add incrementally; a move through $128–$133 would support another small increase. Treat a sustained reclaim of $141–$145 as major confirmation and a reason to move closer to full overweight sizing. Avoid aggressive chasing after vertical moves, and use a break below $108 as a signal to pause additions and reassess. Monitor upcoming company-specific evidence: RPO/billings, AI product adoption, pricing uplift, GAAP operating leverage, SBC, acquisition integration, and free cash flow quality.
