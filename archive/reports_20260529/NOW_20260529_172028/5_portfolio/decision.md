**Investment Thesis**: The final decision on NOW balances the aggressive analyst’s stronger strategic argument with the conservative analyst’s valid tactical timing concerns. The bullish case is compelling: NOW remains a high-quality enterprise software compounder with roughly 22% Q1 revenue growth, about 75% gross margins, $1.53B of quarterly free cash flow, $5.11B of trailing FCF, $8.03B of current deferred revenue, and roughly $2.75B of net cash. The company is not a speculative AI concept; it has mission-critical workflow exposure across IT service management, HR, customer operations, security workflows, automation, governance, and orchestration, which gives it credible relevance if enterprise AI agents increase demand for workflow control layers.

Technically, the aggressive analyst is right that the breakout is meaningful. NOW pushed through the $103–$110 resistance zone on roughly 67M shares, about double the recent 20-session average, and reclaimed the 50-day moving average with positive momentum indicators. That kind of volume should not be ignored and may represent institutional repositioning as the market reassesses the idea that AI is a threat to SaaS rather than a potential demand driver for durable software platforms such as NOW.

However, the conservative and neutral analysts are also right that this is not a clean, unconditional Buy point. The recent 12%–14% one-day move was driven largely by Dell/Snowflake read-throughs and broader AI/software sentiment, not by NOW-specific RPO, billings, guidance, or AI monetization data. NOW is also extended after a roughly 50% rebound from the April low near $83, sits near immediate resistance around $125, faces additional supply at $128–$133 and $136–$142, and remains below the 200-day SMA area around $141–$145. That argues against chasing or immediately sizing to a full overweight.

Valuation supports participation but not overconfidence. The forward P/E near 24.7x, PEG around 1.05, approximately 4% FCF yield, strong FCF conversion, and net cash balance sheet are reasonable for a durable growth compounder. But the trailing P/E around 73.6x, EV/EBITDA near 43.5x, EV/FCF near 24.6x, $547M of Q1 SBC, rising goodwill/intangibles from acquisitions, and only about 2% GAAP net income growth mean the stock remains priced for execution. These risks make sizing and entry discipline critical.

Therefore, the correct portfolio action for NOW is Overweight, not a full Buy and not a passive Hold. The portfolio should participate because business quality, cash generation, AI workflow positioning, and the high-volume technical reversal justify exposure before full consensus confirmation. But the position should be built only through rules-based tranches: start small at proven support, add only after retests of key resistance, and reserve larger sizing for either a sustained reclaim of $141–$145 or company-specific evidence that RPO, billings, AI adoption, operating leverage, and FCF quality are validating the thesis.
