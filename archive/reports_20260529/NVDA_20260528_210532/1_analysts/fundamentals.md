# NVDA Fundamental Research Report — 2026-05-28

## Executive View

NVDA’s latest fundamental data show an exceptionally strong growth, profitability, and cash-generation profile. Revenue, operating income, net income, and free cash flow have all accelerated materially across the most recent quarters, while the balance sheet remains highly liquid with large cash and short-term investment balances, modest debt, and rapidly expanding equity.

The stock is not “cheap” on book value or absolute market capitalization — NVDA has a market cap of approximately **$5.19 trillion** and trades at roughly **33.1x book value** — but the earnings multiple is less demanding relative to growth: **TTM P/E is 32.8x**, **forward P/E is 16.9x**, and **PEG is 0.66**. These valuation metrics suggest that, if forward estimates are credible, the market is still pricing NVDA at a reasonable level relative to its earnings growth trajectory.

The main risks are valuation concentration, high beta, AI infrastructure cyclicality, inventory growth, receivables growth, and the possibility that recent non-operating investment gains inflated reported net income. Still, the operating fundamentals remain extremely strong.

**Actionable stance:** NVDA remains a **BUY**, preferably on pullbacks or through staged accumulation rather than chasing sharp intraday spikes. Existing holders should continue to hold core positions while using risk controls because of the stock’s elevated beta and market-cap sensitivity.
