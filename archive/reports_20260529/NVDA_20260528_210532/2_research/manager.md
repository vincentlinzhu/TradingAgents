**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: The bull side carried the stronger fundamental argument on NVDA: revenue growth around 85% YoY, very high operating margins, massive free cash flow, a net-cash balance sheet, and continued AI infrastructure demand all argue that the long-term thesis remains intact. The forward valuation also looks less extreme than the headline market cap suggests if earnings estimates prove durable. That said, the bear side made valid points on timing and risk: NVDA is crowded, short-term momentum has cooled, inventory is rising faster than revenue, margins are already extremely high, and the stock could sell off if AI sentiment or macro conditions weaken even without company-specific deterioration. So I would not frame NVDA as a risk-free Buy or a name to chase aggressively here. But the evidence is not balanced enough for Hold: the business momentum and platform dominance still outweigh the risks. The right stance is constructive but disciplined: gradually increase exposure on weakness rather than aggressively chase strength.

**Strategic Actions**: Move NVDA to a modest overweight position, not a maximum-sized position. For traders with no position, initiate in stages rather than all at once: add a starter tranche near current levels only if portfolio risk allows, add more near the $208–$210 support area, and reserve additional capital for a potential test of $198–$200 if fundamentals remain intact. For existing holders, maintain the core position and add selectively on pullbacks, but trim only if NVDA is already an outsized single-name exposure. Do not chase aggressively below the $220–$223 resistance/reclaim zone; a sustained move above $220–$223 with improving momentum would justify adding another tranche. Risk controls: reassess the overweight if NVDA decisively breaks below $198–$200, if AI demand indicators deteriorate, if margins begin compressing materially, or if inventory growth starts coinciding with revenue deceleration. Keep sizing disciplined given NVDA’s high beta and crowded positioning.
