## Risk Factors

The main risk is not trend weakness; the main risk is **extension**.

QTUM is:

- Near recent highs,
- Above the 70 RSI threshold,
- Over 20% above the 50 SMA,
- Over 40% above the 200 SMA,
- Experiencing rising ATR.

That creates conditions where even a normal pullback could be sharp. A move from 159.75 back to the 10 EMA / VWMA area near 150–151 would only be a normal technical reset, but it would still represent a decline of roughly **5–6%**.

The worst setup would be a failed breakout: price pushes slightly above 160.71, volume fades, then price closes back below 153–155. That would indicate buyer exhaustion and could trigger a deeper mean-reversion move.
