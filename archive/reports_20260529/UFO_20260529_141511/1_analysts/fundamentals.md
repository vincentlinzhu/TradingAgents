- Rising interest rates.

- Lower defense/aerospace spending expectations.
- Disappointing earnings from key holdings.
- Reduced investor enthusiasm for space-economy narratives.

`UFO` does not look like a deep-value instrument. It is better viewed as a **growth/thematic momentum vehicle**. Traders should avoid treating the ETF as a defensive or income-oriented holding.
