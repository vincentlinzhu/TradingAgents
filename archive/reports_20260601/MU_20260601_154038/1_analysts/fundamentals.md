# 1. Company Profile

Micron Technology, Inc. is one of the world’s major semiconductor memory manufacturers. The company designs, manufactures, and sells memory and storage products, including DRAM, NAND, and other memory-related technologies. These products are used across data centers, AI infrastructure, PCs, smartphones, automotive systems, industrial devices, and consumer electronics.

`MU` is highly cyclical because memory pricing is driven by supply-demand balance. During upcycles, gross margins and free cash flow can expand sharply. During downcycles, revenue, profitability, and cash generation can deteriorate quickly. This makes `MU` more volatile than many other large-cap semiconductor names.
