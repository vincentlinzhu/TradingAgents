## 1. Company Profile

ServiceNow, Inc. (`NOW`) is a large-cap enterprise software company focused on cloud-based workflow automation. Its platform is used by enterprises to digitize and automate IT service management, IT operations, employee workflows, customer workflows, security operations, risk/compliance workflows, and broader business process automation. The company’s strategic positioning remains tied to enterprise digital transformation, platform consolidation, automation, and AI-enabled productivity.

`NOW` has the characteristics of a high-quality SaaS/software platform business: high gross margins, recurring revenue visibility, strong free cash flow, and a net cash/liquidity position. However, the stock also trades at a premium valuation, meaning execution risk and margin trends matter heavily for traders.

### Year-over-Year Comparison: Q1 2026 vs. Q1 2025

Compared with the quarter ended **2025-03-31**:

- Revenue increased from **$3.09B** to **$3.77B**, up approximately **22.1% YoY**
- Gross profit increased from **$2.44B** to **$2.83B**, up approximately **16.1% YoY**
- Operating income increased from **$451M** to **$503M**, up approximately **11.5% YoY**
- Net income increased from **$460M** to **$469M**, up only approximately **2.0% YoY**
- Diluted EPS increased from **$0.44** to **$0.45**
- EBITDA increased from **$721M** to **$937M**, up approximately **30.0% YoY**

The revenue growth rate is strong, but the slower growth in net income is notable. One major reason is the higher tax provision: Q1 2026 tax provision was **$204M**, compared with **$95M** in Q1 2025. The Q1 2026 effective tax rate used in the data was approximately **30%**, materially higher than the **17%** rate in Q1 2025.

### Margin Trend

The company continues to generate attractive software margins, but gross margin compressed materially:

- Q1 2025 gross margin: approximately **78.9%**
- Q1 2026 gross margin: approximately **75.1%**

Cost of revenue rose from **$651M** in Q1 2025 to **$940M** in Q1 2026, up approximately **44.4% YoY**, meaning cost of revenue grew roughly twice as fast as revenue. Traders should monitor whether this reflects temporary investments, infrastructure/AI-related costs, acquisition effects, or a more persistent margin pressure.

Operating expenses grew more moderately:

- R&D increased from **$703M** to **$823M**, up approximately **17.1% YoY**
- SG&A increased from **$1.28B** to **$1.50B**, up approximately **17.2% YoY**
- Selling and marketing increased from **$1.05B** to **$1.22B**, up approximately **15.4% YoY**

This suggests operating expense discipline was better than cost-of-revenue discipline. The key issue is whether gross margin stabilizes.

## 9. Key Risks

1. **Premium valuation:** TTM P/E of approximately **81x** and price/sales near **10x** leave limited room for disappointment.
2. **Gross margin compression:** Cost of revenue grew much faster than revenue in Q1 2026.
3. **Net income growth lagged revenue growth:** Net income rose only about **2% YoY** in Q1 2026 despite revenue growth above **22%**.
4. **High stock-based compensation:** SBC remains meaningful at approximately **14.5% of Q1 revenue**.
5. **Negative working capital:** Current ratio of **0.845** is manageable given deferred revenue, but still worth monitoring.
6. **Acquisition integration risk:** Goodwill and intangibles have increased sharply, suggesting elevated M&A activity.
7. **Buyback timing risk:** Large repurchases can be value-enhancing, but at high valuations they may be less accretive if growth expectations weaken.

## 10. Trading View and Actionable Insights

### Recommendation: **HOLD**

`NOW` remains a high-quality enterprise software compounder with strong revenue growth, excellent free cash flow, and a conservative debt profile. However, the stock’s valuation already discounts substantial future earnings growth. The combination of premium valuation, gross margin compression, heavy SBC, and elevated acquisition activity argues against aggressive new buying at current fundamentals.

### For Existing Holders

A **HOLD** is justified as long as:

- Revenue growth remains around or above the low-20% range
- Deferred revenue continues growing at a healthy pace
- Free cash flow margins remain strong
- Gross margin stabilizes
- Buybacks offset dilution without materially weakening liquidity

### For New Buyers

Traders considering new exposure should wait for one of the following:

- A pullback that improves the risk/reward
- Clear evidence that gross margin compression is temporary
- Stronger operating margin expansion
- Continued acceleration in free cash flow per share
- Technical confirmation if the stock reclaims and holds above its longer-term moving average

### For Short-Term Traders

The stock is fundamentally strong but valuation-sensitive. Earnings, guidance, remaining performance obligations, billings/deferred revenue trends, AI monetization commentary, and margin guidance are likely to drive sharp moves. A negative surprise in billings or gross margin could pressure the stock given the premium multiple.
