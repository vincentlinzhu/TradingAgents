So I would not chase PATH aggressively as if the turnaround is fully proven. But the evidence is no longer balanced: fundamentals, balance sheet, valuation, and technicals have all improved enough to justify leaning constructive. The right stance is to gradually increase exposure while managing entry risk.

**Strategic Actions**: Move PATH to a modest overweight rather than a full-size aggressive long. Build the position in tranches: add 25%–40% of the intended overweight exposure on a pullback into $12.60–$13.00, or on a confirmed close above $13.20 with strong volume. Add another tranche only if PATH holds above the 200-day area and momentum pushes toward $13.60–$14.30, or if the next quarter confirms ARR durability, billings/RPO strength, and margin consistency. Near-term upside targets are $14.00–$14.30, then $15+ if follow-through continues. Keep position sizing disciplined—roughly 1.25x–1.5x a normal software position, not a maximum-conviction allocation, until deferred revenue, AI monetization, and operating leverage show more durability.
