The bear case is mainly a timing and valuation caution, not a thesis-breaker. Because VOO is extended but still technically and structurally strong, the best stance is constructive but disciplined: overweight gradually rather than buy aggressively all at once.

Add a small initial tranche near current levels only if the portfolio is underexposed. Add more on pullbacks into $686–$690, and more meaningfully near $674–$678. If VOO fails at $700 and breaks below $686 with weakening momentum, pause further adds and reassess; a decisive break below the 50-day area around $647 would reduce the overweight case.
