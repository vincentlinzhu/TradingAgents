# Actionable Trading View

## For Existing Longs

**Hold, but tighten risk controls.**

The trend is still bullish, and there is no confirmed bearish reversal. However, DRAM is extended. Consider:

- Holding while price remains above **$63–$64**
- Using a trailing stop based on ATR, for example **2 ATR below recent highs**
- Since ATR is about $3.54, a 2 ATR stop from the $70.15 high would be around **$63.10**
- Partial profit-taking may be reasonable if position size is large after the run

## For New Buyers

**Avoid chasing at current levels.**

A new long entry at $69–$70 has elevated risk because:

- RSI is near 80
- Price is above the upper Bollinger Band
- Price is far above the 10 EMA and 50 SMA
- ATR has expanded, increasing whipsaw risk

Better setups would include:

1. **Pullback entry:** Wait for a controlled pullback toward **$63–$64** or ideally **$59–$61** with stabilization.  
2. **Breakout entry:** Consider only if DRAM closes decisively above **$70.15** with strong volume, but use a tight plan and smaller size.  
3. **Consolidation entry:** Best risk/reward may come after several sideways sessions that allow the 10 EMA and Bollinger Bands to catch up.

## For Short Sellers

Shorting remains risky.

The stock is overbought, but strong trends can stay overbought longer than expected. A bearish trade would require confirmation, such as:

- Failed breakout above $70
- Close below $67
- RSI rolling below 70
- MACD histogram weakening
- Heavy selling volume

Without those signals, shorting DRAM is countertrend.
