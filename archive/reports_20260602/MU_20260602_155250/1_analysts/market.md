# Trading View

## For Existing Longs

**Best stance: HOLD, but trail stops.**

The trend is too strong to justify an automatic sell, but the extension is too large to ignore. Existing holders should consider protecting gains using volatility-adjusted trailing stops.

Possible approaches:

- Aggressive stop: below **$1,006–$1,017**
- Moderate stop: below **$970–$980**
- Wider swing stop: below **$928–$950**
- Trend-following stop: below the 10 EMA, currently near **$876**, though this is very wide

Partial profit-taking may be reasonable for traders with oversized gains, especially if `MU` fails near $1,100.

---

## For New Buyers

**Avoid chasing at current levels.**

The stock is bullish, but buying after a near-vertical move above the upper Bollinger Band and with RSI above 80 creates unfavorable risk/reward.

Better entry setups would include:

1. Pullback toward **$1,000–$1,017** that holds and rebounds  
2. Consolidation between **$970–$1,075** followed by a clean breakout  
3. Retest of the 10 EMA with bullish reversal confirmation  
4. A shallow pullback on declining volume, followed by renewed high-volume buying  

---

## For Short Sellers

Shorting `MU` here is dangerous.

Although the stock is overbought, the trend and momentum are strongly bullish. Overbought can become more overbought in a parabolic move.

A bearish short setup would require confirmation such as:

- Failed breakout above $1,100
- Close back below $1,000
- MACD histogram rolling over
- RSI divergence
- High-volume bearish reversal candle

Without those signals, shorting is countertrend and high risk.

---

# Final Recommendation

`MU` remains in a powerful bullish trend, supported by rising moving averages, strong MACD, positive MACD histogram, and breakout behavior. However, the stock is extremely extended above its 10 EMA, 50 SMA, and 200 SMA, while RSI is above 80 and price is above the Bollinger Upper Band.

Therefore, the best tactical recommendation is:

**HOLD for existing positions, but do not chase new entries at current levels. Use trailing stops or partial profit-taking to manage risk.**
