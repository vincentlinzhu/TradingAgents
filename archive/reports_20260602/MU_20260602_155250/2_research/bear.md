That may work for traders, but it is not a compelling investment case after a nearly **96% one-month move**.

The better discipline is to separate company quality from entry quality. MU may be a strong company. It may even remain a long-term AI-memory winner. But at this exact point, the stock is extended, crowded, volatile, and dependent on optimistic forward assumptions.

Missing another 5–10% upside is not the same as exposing yourself to a plausible **15–25% reset** — or worse if DRAM/HBM expectations soften.
