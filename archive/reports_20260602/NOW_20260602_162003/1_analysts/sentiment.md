- Automating enterprise workflows,

- Expanding average contract value through premium AI features.

This is why bullish commentary suggests the market may not fully appreciate `NOW`’s AI momentum.

### 3.2 But valuation sensitivity remains a major risk

High-quality enterprise software stocks often trade at premium multiples. When rates rise, growth expectations soften, or software ETFs face selling pressure, premium names can fall quickly even without bad company-specific news.

The reported **-6.04% daily decline** is a reminder that `NOW` remains vulnerable to multiple compression.

### 3.3 The AI narrative is no longer one-directional

Earlier AI enthusiasm mostly benefited infrastructure names like Nvidia. The current debate is more complicated for software:

- AI may help software companies sell new tools and increase productivity.
- AI may also reduce the need for some software seats or disrupt existing SaaS pricing models.

ServiceNow’s advantage is that its platform is deeply embedded in enterprise processes. But the market is still sorting out which software firms are AI winners versus AI losers.
