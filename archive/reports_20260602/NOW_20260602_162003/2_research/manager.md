**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: Both sides made credible points on NOW. The bull case carried the stronger long-term business argument: ServiceNow is still growing revenue above 20% at large scale, has roughly 75% gross margins, very strong free cash flow generation, healthy liquidity, sticky enterprise workflow positioning, and a credible path to benefit from AI as a workflow automation platform rather than just another SaaS vendor adding AI features. Those are the traits of a premium compounder. That said, the bear case is not just noise. NOW is still valued for a lot of future execution, GAAP net income growth is lagging revenue growth, SBC and acquisition activity are meaningful, buybacks are mostly offsetting dilution, and the technical setup is not fully repaired with the stock below the 200-day SMA and short-term momentum overbought. My read is that the bear wins the near-term timing argument, but the bull wins the strategic ownership argument. That argues against an aggressive all-at-once Buy, but also against stepping aside entirely. The best conclusion is constructive but disciplined accumulation.

**Strategic Actions**: Gradually increase exposure to NOW rather than chase with a full position immediately. Target a modest overweight position built in tranches: initiate/add a first 25%-35% of intended incremental exposure only if NOW holds the $124-$125 support area; add another 25%-35% on a controlled pullback and stabilization near $118-$120; reserve additional capital for either a deeper but stable pullback near $110-$112 or a confirmed breakout above the $141-$142 200-day SMA zone. Do not add aggressively if NOW breaks $124-$125 on heavy volume; in that case pause new buying and reassess. Existing holders can maintain core exposure but should avoid letting the position become oversized until operating leverage, SBC discipline, and acquisition integration improve. A loss of $98-$100 would materially weaken the recovery setup and should trigger risk reduction. A decisive close above $141-$142 with sector confirmation would justify moving closer to a full overweight allocation, with $150 as the next upside area to monitor.
