## Fundamental Risks

### 1. Elevated Valuation

A **P/E ratio of 36.40** is high. This means the ETF has less room for earnings disappointment. If the underlying companies fail to deliver strong earnings growth, `QQQ` could face valuation compression.

### 2. Low Dividend Yield

The **0.42% dividend yield** offers little income support. In risk-off markets or periods of rising interest rates, low-yield growth assets can underperform.

### 3. Concentration Risk

Although `QQQ` owns many securities, performance is often driven by a relatively small group of mega-cap companies. If a handful of large constituents weaken, the ETF can decline even if many smaller holdings remain stable.

### 4. Interest Rate Sensitivity

Growth stocks are generally more sensitive to changes in discount rates. If long-term rates rise, the present value of future earnings declines, which can pressure high-multiple assets like `QQQ`.

### 5. Near-High Risk

The 52-week high is **746.44**, while the 52-week low is **522.66**. If `QQQ` is trading near the upper end of this range, upside may depend on continued strong earnings momentum and favorable macro conditions.
