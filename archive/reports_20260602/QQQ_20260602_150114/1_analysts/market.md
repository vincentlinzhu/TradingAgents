- **Aggressive trailing stop:** below **735–736**, near recent intraday support and roughly 1 ATR from the latest close.

Avoid chasing aggressively at **746+** unless using a short-term breakout strategy with disciplined risk.

Preferred entry zones:

1. **726–730:** first attractive pullback area  
2. **705–715:** better risk/reward if a deeper correction develops  
3. **655–660:** major medium-term support, though this would require a much larger decline

### For short sellers

Shorting QQQ purely because RSI is overbought is risky. The trend is strongly bullish across the 10 EMA, 50 SMA, and 200 SMA. A bearish trade would need confirmation, such as:

- Failed breakout above 750  
- Close back below 735  
- RSI rollover with price weakness  
- Break below the 10-day EMA  

Until then, shorting is countertrend.
