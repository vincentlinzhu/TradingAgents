So this is not a full-throttle Buy at the current spike. The right conclusion is constructive but disciplined: the long-term thesis has improved enough to favor increasing exposure, but position building should be staged and pullback-sensitive. The bull argument wins on the multi-year direction, while the bear argument wins on not chasing aggressively.

**Strategic Actions**: Move STM to a modest Overweight, not a maximum-size Buy. For existing holders, maintain the core position and avoid aggressive profit-taking unless position size has become excessive; trim only enough to keep risk limits intact after the sharp rally. For new or underweight accounts, build exposure in tranches: initiate or add a partial position only, then add more on controlled pullbacks toward the $72.70–$75.60 zone, with a deeper add window around $69.00–$70.70 if the AI/data-center thesis remains intact and volume is not disorderly. Avoid chasing a full position immediately above the post-gap high near $80.58 unless STM shows several sessions of constructive consolidation or a confirmed breakout with follow-through. Target a position roughly 25%–50% above benchmark weight or normal portfolio weight, rather than an aggressive double-weight allocation. Risk controls: reassess if STM breaks below the $69–$70 area on heavy volume, if data-center guidance is walked back, if gross margin fails to improve over the next few quarters, or if free cash flow remains negative due to capex without clear revenue conversion.
