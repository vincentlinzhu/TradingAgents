## Executive View

`NOW` remains a high-quality enterprise software compounder with strong recurring-revenue economics, high gross margins, substantial free cash flow, and a net-cash balance sheet. Revenue continues to grow at a strong pace, with Q1 2026 revenue up approximately **22% year over year**. The business also generated very strong free cash flow in the latest quarter, with Q1 2026 free cash flow of **$1.529 billion**.

However, the stock’s fundamentals are already priced at premium levels: **TTM P/E of ~70x**, **price/book of ~10.4x**, and **EV/sales of roughly 8.5x** based on available data. While the forward P/E of **~23.5x** and PEG ratio of **1.21** suggest valuation is less stretched on forward estimates, traders should balance this against margin compression, heavy stock-based compensation, acquisition-related goodwill growth, and elevated share repurchases that exceeded free cash flow in the most recent quarter.

**Actionable stance:** `NOW` is fundamentally strong, but not obviously cheap. The preferred trading posture is **HOLD** for existing positions, with selective accumulation only on valuation pullbacks or after confirmation of accelerating growth/margin expansion.

## Company Profile

ServiceNow, Inc. provides cloud-based workflow automation software for enterprises. Its platform is used to digitize, automate, and integrate workflows across IT, employee experience, customer service, security operations, risk/compliance, and industry-specific functions.

Key fundamental characteristics of the business model:

- **Recurring software revenue profile**
- **High gross margins**
- **Strong operating cash flow conversion**
- **Enterprise customer base**
- **Platform expansion opportunity**
- **Material investment in R&D and sales capacity**
- **High stock-based compensation typical of scaled software firms**

`NOW` sits in the enterprise software and workflow automation category, where investor expectations are heavily tied to subscription growth, remaining performance obligations, AI monetization, net retention, and operating margin expansion.

### Liquidity Position

`NOW` had **$5.182B** of cash and short-term investments against **$2.431B** of total debt. This implies a net cash position of roughly:

**$5.182B cash/ST investments - $2.431B debt = $2.751B net cash**

That is a strong liquidity profile.

The reported current ratio of **0.845** and negative working capital of **-$1.548B** may look weak at first glance, but for subscription software businesses this is not necessarily alarming. The key reason is that current liabilities include **$8.030B of current deferred revenue**, which reflects cash collected or contracted before revenue recognition. Deferred revenue is an obligation to deliver services, not a traditional cash debt repayment obligation.

### Balance Sheet Quality

Total debt is manageable relative to equity and cash resources. Debt/equity of **20.7%** is not aggressive.

However, goodwill and intangible assets increased meaningfully. Goodwill and other intangible assets reached **$6.020B** in Q1 2026, up from **$1.535B** in Q1 2025. This indicates significant acquisition activity or purchase accounting adjustments. Traders should monitor whether acquisitions are driving durable growth or creating future impairment risk.

Treasury stock also increased materially, reflecting aggressive share repurchases. Treasury stock was **$5.375B** as of Q1 2026, compared with **$1.513B** in Q1 2025.

## Final Fundamental Assessment

`NOW` is a high-quality software company with strong recurring revenue, a powerful enterprise platform, robust free cash flow, and a net-cash balance sheet. The company continues to grow revenue at an impressive rate and remains profitable on a GAAP basis.

The main issue is valuation. The stock already embeds high expectations, and recent margin trends are mixed. Strong free cash flow helps justify a premium, but traders should demand continued execution.

**Final view:** `NOW` is a **HOLD**. Existing investors can maintain exposure, but new capital should be deployed selectively, preferably on pullbacks or after evidence of accelerating earnings leverage.
