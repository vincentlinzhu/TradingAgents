These strengths argue against Sell and justify maintaining core exposure.

The right stance is controlled participation: keep exposure to NOW’s quality and AI optionality, but make the stock earn additional capital through either a clean $108–$110 support hold with calmer selling pressure or a confirmed reclaim of $140–$142.
