I think the bear wins the short-term entry argument, but not the portfolio allocation argument. QQQ is extended, not broken, and underweighting a strong secular leader before the trend actually fails would be premature. That leads to a constructive but disciplined recommendation: stay above benchmark exposure, but build exposure gradually rather than chase aggressively at resistance.

Risk controls: treat a close below $730 as a warning to pause additions; below $724–$725, trim back toward benchmark weight; below $713–$718, reassess the overweight and consider reducing to Hold/neutral exposure.
