FINAL TRANSACTION PROPOSAL: **HOLD**

## DRAM Technical Trading Report — 2026-06-04

### Executive view

DRAM remains in a powerful uptrend, but the risk/reward has become less attractive after a very sharp advance and a high-volume reversal/pullback on 2026-06-04. The stock closed at **$65.70**, down from **$69.71** on 2026-06-03, after recently touching the **$70.15** area. Despite the pullback, DRAM is still trading well above its short- and medium-term trend measures, including the 10 EMA, VWMA, and 50 SMA.

The technical picture is best described as: **strong bullish trend, but overextended and vulnerable to near-term consolidation.** For existing positions, the trend does not yet justify a full exit, but chasing new longs at current levels is risky. Hence the recommendation is **HOLD**, with tighter risk controls and partial profit-taking considered for short-term traders.

## Trend analysis

### 1. Short-term trend: still bullish, but stretched

The **10 EMA** was **$62.61** as of 2026-06-03. DRAM closed at **$65.70** on 2026-06-04, which remains above that short-term trend reference.

That matters because the 10 EMA has acted as a rising short-term support guide during the recent advance. DRAM has moved sharply above this average during the rally, but the 2026-06-04 pullback brought price closer to the short-term trend line without decisively breaking it.

From a trading perspective:

- As long as DRAM holds above the rising 10 EMA area, the immediate uptrend remains intact.
- A decisive close below the 10 EMA would be the first warning that momentum is cooling more seriously.
- A break below the 2026-06-04 low of **$63.18** would increase the risk of a deeper retracement.

### 2. Medium-term trend: very strong

The **50 SMA** was **$45.28** as of 2026-06-03. DRAM is still far above that level.

This confirms that the medium-term trend remains strongly bullish. However, the distance between price and the 50 SMA is also a warning that the stock is extended. A stock can stay extended in a powerful momentum phase, but the farther price moves above its medium-term average, the more vulnerable it becomes to sharp pullbacks.

A move back toward the 50 SMA would be severe from current levels, so this is not a near-term trading stop. Rather, the 50 SMA is best used as evidence that DRAM remains in a larger bullish regime.

## Trading interpretation

### For existing long positions

A **HOLD** stance is appropriate, but risk should be actively managed. The trend remains bullish, price is still above the 10 EMA and VWMA, and MACD remains positive. However, the combination of overbought RSI, elevated ATR, and high-volume selling on 2026-06-04 argues against complacency.

Possible actions:

- Hold core position while DRAM remains above **$62.50–$63.00**.
- Consider partial profit-taking if position size is large or gains are substantial.
- Use a trailing stop based on ATR or recent swing lows.
- Watch for a close below **$63.18** as a near-term warning.
- A close below **$58.50–$60.50** would materially weaken the bullish setup.

### For new buyers

New entries are not favored immediately after the current move. The better approach is to wait for one of two setups:

1. **Pullback entry**
   - Look for stabilization near **$62.50–$63.00** or **$58.50–$60.50**.
   - Prefer confirmation through a bullish reversal candle or strong close.

2. **Breakout entry**
   - Look for a close above **$70.15** with strong volume.
   - Avoid buying a weak intraday spike that fails back below resistance.

### For short-term traders

The stock is volatile enough that tactical traders may trade around the position, but shorting aggressively remains risky because the primary trend is still up. A short setup would only become more attractive if DRAM loses the 10 EMA area, breaks the 2026-06-04 low, and shows continued MACD histogram deterioration.

---

## Final recommendation

**HOLD** is the most balanced transaction proposal.

DRAM is still technically bullish, but the stock is extended and showing early signs of short-term exhaustion. The trend has not broken, so a sell call is premature. At the same time, the risk/reward is no longer attractive enough for a fresh buy at current levels.

A stronger buy setup would require either a healthy pullback into support or a confirmed breakout above the recent high near **$70.15**. A downgrade toward sell would become more likely if DRAM closes below **$58.50–$60.50** with expanding downside volume.
