## Executive Summary

Micron Technology, Inc. (`MU`) is showing a major fundamental inflection, with exceptionally strong revenue, margin, earnings, and cash-flow expansion in the most recent reported quarter. The company appears to be benefiting from a powerful semiconductor memory upcycle, likely driven by high-bandwidth memory, AI infrastructure demand, tighter supply conditions, and improved pricing across DRAM/NAND markets.

The latest quarterly data show `MU` generated **$23.86B in revenue**, **$17.76B in gross profit**, **$16.14B in operating income**, and **$13.79B in net income** for the quarter ended **2026-02-28**. Margins expanded dramatically, with gross margin reaching approximately **74.4%**, operating margin around **67.6%**, and net margin around **57.8%**. These are very strong levels for a historically cyclical memory manufacturer.

Balance sheet quality has also improved. `MU` held **$14.59B in cash, equivalents, and short-term investments** against **$10.80B in total debt**, implying a net cash position based on reported cash and debt figures. Total debt has declined sequentially, while stockholders’ equity rose sharply to **$72.46B**. Liquidity is strong, with a reported **current ratio of 2.897**.

Cash generation is also improving meaningfully. Operating cash flow reached **$11.90B** in the latest quarter, while free cash flow was **$5.52B**, despite heavy capital expenditures of **$6.39B**. This suggests the company is able to fund aggressive capacity and technology investment while still generating substantial excess cash.

Valuation is mixed. On a trailing basis, `MU` trades at a **TTM P/E of 47.1x**, which looks expensive, but the **forward P/E of 9.4x** and **PEG ratio of 0.37** suggest the market expects very large earnings growth. The main concerns are cyclicality, a very strong recent share-price move, a high **beta of 2.17**, and an elevated **price-to-book ratio of 15.5x**.

Overall, the fundamental trend supports a **BUY** rating for traders and investors willing to tolerate volatility. However, position sizing should reflect the cyclicality and high beta of the stock.

# Company Profile

Micron Technology, Inc. is a leading global semiconductor company specializing in memory and storage products. Its core products include DRAM, NAND, and related memory/storage solutions used in data centers, AI infrastructure, PCs, mobile devices, automotive electronics, industrial applications, and networking hardware.

`MU` is highly exposed to memory market cycles. Revenue, margins, earnings, and free cash flow can fluctuate significantly depending on supply-demand balance, wafer capacity, pricing trends, end-market inventory levels, and capital expenditure cycles. In the current data set, `MU` appears to be in a strong cyclical upswing.

# Valuation Analysis

## Positive Valuation Signals

`MU`’s **forward P/E of 9.4x** is attractive for a company showing rapid revenue and profit growth. The **PEG ratio of 0.37** also suggests that expected earnings growth is not fully reflected in the valuation, assuming forecasts are accurate.

The company’s **TTM EPS of $21.16** compared with **forward EPS of $105.95** implies a major expected earnings step-up. This is consistent with the recent quarterly acceleration, where diluted EPS rose to **$12.07** in the quarter ended 2026-02-28.

## Negative Valuation Signals

The **TTM P/E of 47.1x** is high, meaning the stock is expensive relative to trailing earnings. The **price-to-book ratio of 15.5x** is also elevated, especially for a capital-intensive semiconductor manufacturer. That may indicate the market is pricing in sustained super-cycle profitability.

The stock has also traded in a very wide range, with a **52-week low of $103.38** and a **52-week high of $1,089.29** based on the retrieved data. The 50-day average of **$595.71** is substantially above the 200-day average of **$352.78**, suggesting very strong momentum but also potentially stretched technical conditions.

## Valuation Takeaway

The valuation is attractive on forward earnings but expensive on trailing and book-value metrics. For traders, the key question is whether the current earnings surge is sustainable. If the memory pricing cycle remains strong, the stock can justify higher levels. If pricing weakens, the downside risk is meaningful.

For growth-oriented traders, `MU` is a **BUY**, but entries should be disciplined because of volatility. Pullbacks toward major moving averages or after short-term profit-taking may offer better risk/reward than chasing vertical moves.

Traders should monitor:

- Gross margin sustainability

- AI/high-bandwidth memory demand commentary
- Capex plans
- Inventory levels
- Accounts receivable collection
