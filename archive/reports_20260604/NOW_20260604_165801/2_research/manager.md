**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: The bull case carried the debate, but not strongly enough for an outright Buy. The strongest bullish points are that NOW remains a high-quality enterprise workflow platform growing revenue above 20% at nearly $14B of TTM scale, with strong deferred revenue visibility, 75% gross margins, a 40%+ Q1 free-cash-flow margin, and a net cash balance. The AI angle also looks credible because NOW is applying AI to workflow automation, incident response, IT service management, and employee workflows rather than relying on a vague AI narrative. On forward valuation and free cash flow, the stock is not cheap, but it is not obviously excessive for this quality of business. The bear side made important points: trailing valuation is still rich, Q1 revenue growth did not flow cleanly into GAAP earnings, SBC is high, buybacks exceeded quarterly FCF, acquisition activity has increased, and the chart remains below a declining 200-day moving average. Those concerns argue against chasing aggressively and keep this from being a high-conviction Buy. But the bear argument is more about timing, confirmation, and margin of safety, while the bull argument is anchored in durable business quality, cash generation, and a plausible AI workflow expansion runway. Net: NOW deserves gradual accumulation, but with disciplined sizing and technical risk controls.

**Strategic Actions**: Move NOW to a modest Overweight rather than a full aggressive Buy. For an existing position, add gradually toward a 1.25x–1.5x normal position size, not all at once. For new exposure, build in thirds: start a partial position near current levels only if NOW holds above the 111–115 support zone; add a second tranche on a reclaim of 124–128 with improving volume; reserve the final tranche for a decisive move above 140–142, which would confirm a repair of the long-term trend. Do not chase vertical strength before confirmation. If NOW breaks below 111–112, pause additions and reassess. If it loses 103–105, trim back toward neutral/Hold sizing. If it breaks the 98–99 50-day area, cut the overweight stance and protect capital. Monitor the next earnings reports for GAAP operating margin expansion, EPS growth catching up to revenue growth, SBC as a percentage of revenue, buybacks staying closer to FCF, organic versus acquisition-driven growth, and clearer evidence that AI products are improving ARPU, net retention, or deal velocity.
