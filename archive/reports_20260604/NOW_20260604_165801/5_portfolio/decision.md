Valuation is premium but not disqualifying for this quality: forward P/E around 23.7x, PEG around 1.24, and price/FCF around 24–27x are reasonable if NOW continues compounding revenue above 20% and begins showing better AI monetization and operating leverage.

The balanced portfolio action is therefore staged accumulation in NOW. A second tranche is justified only if NOW reclaims and holds 124–128 with volume, while any move toward 1.25x–1.5x normal exposure should wait until a decisive reclaim and hold above 140–142, where the long-term trend begins to repair. Monitor upcoming earnings for GAAP margin expansion, EPS growth catching up to revenue growth, SBC as a percentage of revenue, buybacks staying closer to free cash flow, organic versus acquisition-driven growth, and proof that AI products are improving ARPU, retention, or deal velocity.
