Bull, you’re right that QQQ is not broken. You’re right that it owns dominant growth companies. You’re right that AI may remain a major long-term theme.

But none of that means investors should ignore price, valuation, positioning, and risk/reward.

At **~$740**, QQQ is priced for a lot to go right:

- Continued AI enthusiasm
- Continued mega-cap earnings strength
- Contained rates
- Benign geopolitics
- Stabilizing semiconductors
- No meaningful rotation out of growth
- Investors continuing to pay premium multiples

That is a crowded list of assumptions.

So my final bear stance is simple:

**Respect QQQ as a long-term vehicle, but do not chase it here. The ETF is extended, expensive, sentiment-sensitive, and vulnerable to mean reversion. Existing holders should tighten risk controls or hedge; new buyers should wait for a better entry or a confirmed breakout.**

QQQ may be a great ETF — but right now, it is priced like one. That is exactly the problem.
