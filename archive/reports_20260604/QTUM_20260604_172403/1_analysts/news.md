## 5. Main Risks to Monitor

### 5.1 Valuation Risk

Quantum companies often trade more on future potential than current earnings. If investors become less willing to pay for long-duration technology stories, `QTUM` could underperform.

### 5.2 Commercialization Risk

The recent narrative says quantum is approaching commercial viability, but broad adoption is still early. Delays, technical setbacks, or disappointing contract monetization could hurt sentiment.

### 5.3 Concentration Risk

Even as an ETF, `QTUM` may have meaningful exposure to a limited set of high-beta quantum and semiconductor names. Sharp reversals in major holdings could move the ETF materially.

### 5.4 Macro Risk

High-growth technology themes are sensitive to interest rates, liquidity, and risk appetite. Any macro shock that pressures duration assets could weigh on `QTUM`.

### 5.5 “Sell-the-News” Risk

The Department of Commerce funding headlines and Quantinuum IPO may already be reflected in prices. If follow-through disappoints, the sector could retrace.
