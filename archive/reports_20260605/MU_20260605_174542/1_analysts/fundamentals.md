## Executive View

Micron Technology, Inc. — **MU** — is showing a dramatic cyclical upswing in fundamentals, with quarterly revenue, margins, earnings, operating cash flow, and free cash flow all accelerating sharply. The latest reported quarter, **2026-02-28**, was exceptionally strong: revenue reached **$23.86B**, net income reached **$13.79B**, operating cash flow reached **$11.90B**, and free cash flow reached **$5.52B**.

The balance sheet has also improved meaningfully. Current liquidity is strong, with a reported **current ratio of 2.897** and working capital of **$27.12B**.

However, the stock already reflects substantial optimism. The reported **forward P/E of 8.15x** and **PEG ratio of 0.37** suggest earnings expectations are extremely strong, but the key trading question is whether these expectations are sustainable through the memory cycle.

**Bottom line:** MU’s fundamentals are very strong and improving, but after a likely major stock re-rating, the risk/reward is more balanced. Existing holders can continue to hold, but new buyers should be selective and look for pullbacks or confirmation that recent margin levels are durable.
