**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: Both sides agree MU’s company fundamentals have dramatically improved: revenue, margins, cash flow, balance sheet quality, and Nvidia HBM4 certification all support the view that MU is becoming a more important AI memory supplier. The bull case is strongest on the medium-term earnings reset: if HBM scarcity and AI-driven DRAM demand persist, MU’s forward valuation looks attractive and the latest selloff may prove to be a sector-driven volatility reset rather than a thesis break. However, the bear case carries the decision today because the question is not whether MU is a better business, but whether the stock offers attractive risk/reward after a near-tripling in roughly two months. MU has suffered a high-volume breakdown, closed at the low, lost the 10-day EMA and VWMA, remains extremely extended above the 50-day and 200-day averages, and is trading inside a fragile AI semiconductor tape where good company-specific news was overwhelmed by sector de-risking. The forward P/E argument is compelling, but it relies on unusually high memory margins and aggressive forward EPS durability; if investors start viewing current profitability as peak-cycle, MU could face both estimate cuts and multiple compression. I do not think the evidence justifies a full Sell because the fundamental thesis is not broken and HBM4 validation is real, but the bear’s timing and risk/reward concerns are strong enough that adding here is premature. The right stance is cautious: reduce exposure and wait for technical confirmation or a better support test.

**Strategic Actions**: Trim MU exposure rather than add into the current breakdown. For existing positions, reduce to a smaller core position sized for high volatility, roughly 50–70% of the intended long-term allocation depending on mandate risk tolerance. Do not initiate new full-size longs at current levels. Rebuild exposure only if MU either reclaims the $951–$962 zone on strong volume, which would signal momentum repair, or successfully tests and reverses from the $820–$825 support area with improving semiconductor breadth. If MU breaks below $820 without a clear reversal, continue trimming and watch downside levels around $795–$803 and $747–$766; a deeper sector unwind could pull MU toward the 50-day area near $607–$608. Monitor upcoming data closely: HBM allocation tied to Nvidia platforms, DRAM/HBM pricing, gross margin durability, receivables conversion, capex discipline, and sector/rate conditions. Maintain flexibility to upgrade back to Hold or Overweight if technical confirmation arrives while fundamentals remain intact.
