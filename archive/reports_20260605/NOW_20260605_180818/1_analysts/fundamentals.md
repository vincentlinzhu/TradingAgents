# ServiceNow, Inc. (`NOW`) Fundamental Report  
**Current date:** 2026-06-05

**Company:** ServiceNow, Inc.

## 1. Executive View

ServiceNow (`NOW`) remains a high-quality enterprise software company with strong recurring-revenue characteristics, robust free cash flow generation, a net-cash-like balance sheet when cash and short-term investments are compared with total debt, and continued double-digit revenue growth. The company’s fundamentals support a premium valuation.

However, the investment setup is not without risk. `NOW` trades at a rich trailing valuation, with a **TTM P/E of ~66.9x**, **price-to-book of ~9.9x**, and an estimated **EV/sales above 8x**. Revenue growth remains strong, but recent margin trends show some pressure: gross margin declined year over year in Q1 2026, while net income growth was modest relative to revenue growth. The balance sheet also shows negative working capital, mainly due to large deferred revenue obligations, which is common for subscription software businesses but still worth monitoring.

**Bottom line:** `NOW` is fundamentally strong, but much of that quality appears reflected in the valuation. For traders and investors, the current setup favors **HOLD** rather than aggressive buying unless valuation improves or growth reaccelerates with margin expansion.

---

## 2. Company Profile

ServiceNow (`NOW`) is a major enterprise cloud software provider focused on workflow automation, digital transformation, IT service management, customer service management, HR workflows, security operations, and increasingly AI-enabled enterprise productivity tools.

The company operates in the **Technology** sector and the **Software – Application** industry. Its business model is largely subscription-based, which typically creates:

- High recurring revenue visibility.
- Large deferred revenue balances.
- Strong cash flow conversion.
- High gross margins.
- Significant sales and R&D investment to sustain growth.

ServiceNow’s platform position gives it meaningful enterprise relevance, but its stock valuation requires continued execution.

## 13. Actionable Trading Insights

### For existing holders

**Hold.** The fundamentals remain strong enough to justify continued ownership, especially for long-term investors focused on enterprise software growth and free cash flow.

### For new buyers

Avoid chasing unless one or more of the following occurs:

- Valuation pulls back toward a more attractive free cash flow yield.
- Revenue growth remains above 20% with improving margins.
- Management demonstrates that acquisitions are accelerating platform growth.
- Gross margin stabilizes or improves.

### For short-term traders

Key near-term signals to watch:

1. **Revenue growth vs expectations**  
   If growth falls meaningfully below 20%, valuation could compress.

2. **Operating margin trend**  
   Margin expansion would support multiple resilience.

3. **Deferred revenue growth**  
   Current deferred revenue is a key indicator of future subscription revenue.

4. **Free cash flow conversion**  
   Sustained FCF margins above 30% would be bullish.

5. **Share repurchase pace**  
   Continued large buybacks may support EPS but could also reduce balance sheet flexibility.

### Potential bullish trigger

A strong upcoming quarter showing:

- Revenue growth above 20%.
- Operating margin expansion.
- Strong remaining performance obligations or deferred revenue.
- Continued high free cash flow.

### Potential bearish trigger

A quarter showing:

- Revenue growth deceleration.
- Further gross margin compression.
- Rising SBC without EPS leverage.
- Weak billings or deferred revenue growth.
- Acquisition-related integration issues.

## 14. Recommendation Rationale

The recommendation is **HOLD**.

`NOW` is a fundamentally strong company with excellent revenue growth, strong cash flow, and a healthy balance sheet. However, valuation remains demanding, and recent margin trends do not yet justify a more aggressive **BUY** recommendation at current levels.

The stock is best viewed as a high-quality compounder that should be accumulated opportunistically during valuation pullbacks rather than chased after premium multiple expansion.
