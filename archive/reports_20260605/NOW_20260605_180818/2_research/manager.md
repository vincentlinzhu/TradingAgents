**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: For NOW, the bull side made the stronger long-term business-quality argument, but the bear side raised enough near-term and valuation concerns to keep this from being a full Buy. The positives are substantial: ServiceNow is still growing revenue above 20% at nearly $14B of TTM scale, generated $1.529B of Q1 free cash flow at a 40.6% margin, has roughly $2.75B of net cash/investments, and benefits from a sticky enterprise workflow position that should be strategically relevant as AI governance, incident response, employee workflows, and automation move into production environments. Partnerships around AI Control Tower and incident response support the view that NOW is not just an AI narrative stock; it has real workflow distribution. That said, the bear case correctly highlights that the stock is not cheap on trailing numbers, GAAP profit leverage was weak with only about 2% net income growth, gross and operating margins compressed, SBC is material, goodwill/acquisition risk is rising, and the chart remains technically fragile below the 200-day moving average. The key distinction is that the bear case is mostly about timing, entry discipline, and proof points, while the bull case is about durable platform quality and cash-flow compounding. I would not chase NOW aggressively here, but I do think the business strength and AI workflow positioning justify gradually increasing exposure on weakness or confirmation. That leads to an Overweight recommendation rather than Buy or Hold.

**Strategic Actions**: Implement a phased Overweight in NOW rather than an immediate full-size buy. For an existing benchmark position, increase exposure modestly by 25–50 bps now or on stabilization above the 108–112 support area, with room to add another tranche if NOW reclaims 119–121 on strong volume. For new capital, build gradually: initiate a half-position near current support, reserve additional capital for either a pullback that holds 103–105 or a confirmed breakout above 119–121. Do not chase into the 124–128 or 132–136 resistance zones without evidence of improving momentum. Use 98–100 as a major risk-control level; a sustained break below that area should trigger a reassessment and likely reduction back toward market weight. Monitor the next earnings report specifically for margin stabilization, AI-related monetization evidence, billings/deferred revenue strength, SBC dilution, and acquisition integration commentary. Target position sizing should be above benchmark but not maximum conviction until GAAP margins and the technical trend improve.
