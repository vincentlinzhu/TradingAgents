### 2. Low income cushion

The dividend yield is very low. Investors are not being compensated meaningfully through cash distributions while waiting for capital gains.

High-duration growth assets tend to be sensitive to real interest rates. If rates rise, the present value of future earnings may decline, pressuring valuation multiples.

QQQ appears to be closer to its 52-week high than its 52-week low. That does not mean it cannot go higher, but it does reduce the immediate risk/reward for new entries.
