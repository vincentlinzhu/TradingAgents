### Interpretation

A P/E ratio of **32.21x** implies that investors are paying a relatively high price for each dollar of earnings generated by the ETF’s underlying holdings. This can be justified if earnings growth remains strong, but it leaves less margin of safety if:

- AI or quantum computing enthusiasm moderates;
- semiconductor demand slows;
- interest rates remain high;
- high-growth technology multiples compress;
