## 6. Bear Case for QTUM

The bear case is mostly about timing and excess enthusiasm:

- QTUM has already moved sharply in 2026.  
- Quantum computing remains an early-stage theme with uncertain commercialization timelines.  
- Many quantum-related companies are volatile and may trade more on expectations than current earnings.  
- Government funding headlines may not translate quickly into revenue.  
- If risk appetite weakens, high-growth thematic ETFs like QTUM may underperform.  
- A crowded momentum trade can reverse quickly if investors take profits.

The biggest near-term concern is not that the quantum theme is invalid, but that QTUM may be extended after a strong run.
