## Trading Strategy

### For Existing Longs

**Recommendation: HOLD, but manage risk actively.**

A full exit is not yet technically required because MU remains above the 50 SMA, 200 SMA, VWMA, and likely still near or above the short-term EMA after the 2026-06-08 rebound. However, the stock has clearly entered a higher-risk phase.

Suggested approach:

- Hold core position while MU remains above **$864–$882**.
- Consider trimming partial profits if MU fails near **$996–$1,035**.
- Add only if MU consolidates constructively or reclaims **$1,035+** with strong volume.
- Reassess aggressively if MU closes below **$864**.

### For New Buyers

**Avoid chasing immediately.**

A new entry at $949.28 has unfavorable risk/reward unless the trader is using a very short timeframe. The stock is still far above the 50 SMA and 200 SMA, volatility is high, and momentum has recently rolled over.

Better entry setups would be:

1. **Bullish continuation entry:**  
   Buy only after MU closes above **$1,035–$1,080** with strong volume and maintains that breakout.

2. **Pullback entry:**  
   Watch for stabilization near **$864–$882** or possibly **$795–$820**, ideally with declining sell volume and RSI holding above 45–50.

3. **Mean-reversion caution:**  
   Do not assume the first bounce after a sharp decline is safe. Parabolic leaders often produce multiple sharp rallies during broader consolidations.

### For Short Sellers

Shorting MU is risky because the larger trend remains strongly bullish. A short setup would become more credible only if:

- MU fails below **$864**,
- MACD continues rolling over,
- price remains below the 10 EMA,
- and rebound attempts fail below **$996**.

Until then, shorting is countertrend.
