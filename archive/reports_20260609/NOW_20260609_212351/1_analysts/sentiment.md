## Fundamental Sentiment: What the Market Wants to See Next

The market appears to be asking three big questions about `NOW`:

### 1. Can ServiceNow convert AI excitement into measurable revenue?

Investors do not simply want AI product announcements. They want evidence that AI features are increasing average contract values, improving renewal rates, expanding customer adoption, or accelerating new enterprise wins.

### 2. Are deal delays temporary or structural?

If enterprise deal delays are caused mainly by macro uncertainty, sentiment could recover quickly when rates fall or budgets improve. If delays reflect saturation, competition, or buyer hesitation around SaaS pricing, the pressure could last longer.

### 3. Can margins remain healthy while ServiceNow invests in AI and acquisitions?

Integration costs and AI investment can be acceptable if they strengthen the long-term platform. But if costs rise while growth slows, investors may question the premium valuation.
