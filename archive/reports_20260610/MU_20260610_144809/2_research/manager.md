**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: Both sides agree on the core facts: MU’s fundamentals are exceptionally strong, with explosive revenue growth, extraordinary margins, strong free cash flow, debt reduction, and clear participation in AI-driven memory demand. The bull makes the stronger medium-term fundamental argument: MU is not just recovering; earnings power has inflected sharply, inventory is not flashing deterioration, cash conversion is strong, and the forward multiple looks low if even a meaningful portion of the current earnings base holds. That argues against exiting or avoiding MU entirely.

That said, the bear made a persuasive case that the current entry is no longer clean. MU has moved parabolically, is far above key moving averages, has heavy two-way volume, a bearish MACD crossover, ATR near 8% of price, and is sitting between support and upside confirmation. Memory remains cyclical, current margins may be closer to peak than trough, forward EPS is doing a lot of valuation work, and capex/competition could eventually pressure returns. The strongest conclusion is therefore constructive but not full-aggression: own the upcycle, but respect that the risk/reward at the current price does not justify an immediate full-size Buy. The bull wins on fundamentals, while the bear wins on entry discipline and sizing. That supports an Overweight rather than outright Buy or Hold.

**Strategic Actions**: Maintain or build a moderately above-benchmark position in MU, but do not chase with a full allocation immediately. For existing holders, keep a core MU position and trim only if it has become outsized from the recent parabolic move; use hedges or partial profit-taking if portfolio risk limits are stretched. For new or underweight accounts, initiate with a partial position, roughly 40–60% of intended MU exposure, then add only on disciplined triggers.

Execution levels: add selectively if MU holds the $900–920 support/VWMA area with improving volume; stop adding and reassess if MU loses $854–864 decisively. A breakdown below $854 should trigger risk reduction toward benchmark or below-benchmark exposure, with $790–800 as the next downside zone to watch. Conversely, add on confirmed strength if MU reclaims $990–1,000, and only move toward a full overweight if it breaks back above the $1,079–1,089 prior-high zone with constructive volume.

Risk controls: size MU assuming high daily volatility; avoid leverage; keep position sizing below what would be used for a lower-beta compounder. Monitor upcoming quarters for margin sustainability, DRAM/NAND/HBM pricing, receivables collection, inventory trends, capex guidance, and evidence of durable AI memory demand. If forward EPS estimates begin falling materially or gross margins show clear normalization, downgrade exposure quickly.
