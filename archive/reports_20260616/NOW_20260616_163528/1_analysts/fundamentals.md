# Fundamental Report: ServiceNow, Inc. — `NOW`  
**Current date:** 2026-06-16  
**Sector:** Technology

## Executive Summary

ServiceNow, Inc. (`NOW`) remains a high-quality enterprise software company with strong recurring revenue characteristics, high gross margins, expanding free cash flow, and a net-cash balance sheet. The company continues to grow revenue at a healthy pace, with the latest reported quarter showing **$3.77B in revenue**, up approximately **22% year over year** from **$3.09B** in the comparable quarter.

However, the current fundamental setup supports a **HOLD** rather than a fresh aggressive BUY. The business is strong, but valuation remains demanding on trailing metrics, with a **TTM P/E of 60.3x** and **price-to-book of 8.9x**. Forward valuation is more reasonable, with a **forward P/E of 20.2x** and **PEG ratio of 0.99**, but investors should monitor margin trends, stock-based compensation, acquisition-related goodwill growth, and the recent decline in working capital.

For traders, `NOW` looks fundamentally sound but not risk-free. The strongest support for the stock is its **$5.11B TTM free cash flow**, **high gross profit base**, and **net cash position**. The main caution is that recent growth in costs, goodwill, and buybacks may pressure flexibility if revenue growth slows.

---

## Company Profile

ServiceNow, Inc. (`NOW`) is an enterprise software company focused on digital workflow automation. The company provides cloud-based platforms used by enterprises to automate IT, employee, customer, security, and operational workflows. Its platform is typically mission-critical for large organizations, giving the business attractive recurring revenue, customer stickiness, and long-term expansion opportunities.

### Key Business Characteristics

- Enterprise SaaS / cloud software model.
- High gross margin profile.
- Significant subscription and deferred revenue base.
- Strong operating cash flow and free cash flow generation.
- Large addressable market across IT service management, workflow automation, AI-enabled enterprise productivity, customer service, HR, and operations.

## Valuation Analysis

`NOW` trades at a premium valuation on trailing earnings but appears more reasonable on forward earnings expectations.

### Positives

- **Forward P/E of 20.2x** is much lower than the **TTM P/E of 60.3x**, implying the market expects significant earnings growth.
- **PEG ratio of 0.99** suggests valuation is broadly aligned with expected growth.
- **Free cash flow of $5.11B** against a **$104.50B market cap** implies an approximate **FCF yield of 4.9%**, which is attractive for a high-growth software company.

### Concerns

- **TTM P/E of 60.3x** leaves limited room for disappointment.
- **Price/book of 8.91x** reflects a premium multiple.
- Stock performance may be sensitive to revenue deceleration, margin compression, or weaker enterprise software spending.

### Trader Takeaway

`NOW` is not obviously cheap on trailing earnings, but forward estimates and cash flow generation help justify part of the premium. Traders should treat valuation as supportive only if growth and margin execution remain intact.

### Liquidity

The reported current ratio is **0.845**, and working capital was **negative $1.55B** as of 2026 Q1.

At first glance, negative working capital may look concerning. However, for SaaS companies like `NOW`, a large portion of current liabilities is deferred revenue. Current deferred revenue was **$8.03B**, representing customer billings collected or contracted before revenue recognition. This can be structurally positive because customers often pay upfront.

Still, traders should monitor the trend. Working capital declined from:

- **$1.01B positive** in 2025 Q1
- **$28M positive** in 2025 Q4
- **-$1.55B negative** in 2026 Q1

This deterioration is not automatically alarming but deserves attention.

### Debt and Net Cash

Total debt was **$2.43B**, while cash plus short-term investments were **$5.18B**. That implies `NOW` holds more liquid financial resources than total debt, giving it a **net cash-like position**.

This is a major fundamental strength.

### Goodwill and Acquisition Activity

Goodwill rose meaningfully to **$4.54B** in 2026 Q1 from **$1.31B** in 2025 Q1. Other intangibles also rose to **$1.48B** from **$230M** over the same period.

This increase indicates acquisition activity and/or intangible asset additions. Acquisition-driven expansion can support growth, but it also introduces integration risk and potential future impairment risk if acquired assets underperform.

### Balance Sheet Takeaway

`NOW` has a strong balance sheet overall, supported by high cash and investments relative to debt. The main balance sheet issues to watch are negative working capital, rising goodwill/intangibles, and the pace of share repurchases relative to free cash flow.

Operating cash flow was **$1.67B** in 2026 Q1, slightly lower than **$1.68B** in 2025 Q1 but still very strong.

The company repurchased **$2.23B** of stock in 2026 Q1. This was larger than quarterly free cash flow of **$1.53B**, meaning repurchases exceeded internally generated free cash flow during the quarter.

Stock-based compensation was **$547M** in 2026 Q1, equal to approximately **14.5% of quarterly revenue**. This is material.

While common for high-growth software companies, high SBC reduces the quality of GAAP earnings and creates dilution pressure. Buybacks help offset this, but they consume cash.

### Cash Flow Takeaway

Cash generation is one of `NOW`’s strongest fundamentals. However, traders should monitor the relationship between free cash flow, buybacks, acquisitions, and SBC. The company is generating significant cash, but it is also deploying it aggressively.

## Actionable Trading Insights

A bullish view is supported if:

- Revenue continues growing above 20%.
- Free cash flow remains above $5B annualized.
- Operating margin expands from the current low-teens level.
- Deferred revenue remains strong.
- Forward earnings estimates continue rising.

A breakout or accumulation strategy may be justified if the market rewards high-quality software names and `NOW` continues delivering strong quarterly growth.

### For Neutral Traders

A HOLD stance is appropriate because:

- The business is fundamentally strong.
- Cash flow is excellent.
- Balance sheet risk is low.
- But valuation is not deeply discounted.
- Margin trends and SBC require monitoring.

### For Bearish Traders

A bearish setup could emerge if:

- Revenue growth decelerates meaningfully.
- Gross margins continue compressing.
- SBC remains elevated without proportional earnings growth.
- Acquisitions fail to contribute to growth.
- Buybacks continue exceeding free cash flow.

## Recommendation: HOLD

`NOW` is a fundamentally strong company with excellent revenue growth, high gross margins, strong free cash flow, and a healthy balance sheet. However, valuation remains demanding on trailing earnings, and there are enough concerns around margin compression, stock-based compensation, acquisition-related goodwill, and aggressive capital returns to avoid a strong BUY rating at this time.

The best fundamental stance is **HOLD**.

Traders already long `NOW` can continue holding if revenue growth and free cash flow remain strong. New buyers may prefer to wait for either a valuation reset or stronger evidence of operating margin expansion.
