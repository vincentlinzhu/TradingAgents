**Recommendation**: Overweight

**Rationale**: The bull side carried the stronger long-term business argument on NOW: ServiceNow remains a scaled, mission-critical enterprise workflow platform growing revenue around 22% YoY, generating roughly $5.11B of TTM free cash flow, and showing a credible AI monetization path through Now Assist and a reported $1.5B 2026 AI revenue target. The IBM, Wipro, and Cognizant partnerships are not proof of revenue yet, but they do strengthen the go-to-market and implementation ecosystem for enterprise AI workflows. Valuation is not cheap on trailing earnings, but the forward P/E around 20x, PEG near 1.0, and roughly 4.9% FCF yield are reasonable for this quality and growth profile. That said, the bear side is right that NOW is not a clean technical buy: the stock is below the 10-day EMA, VWMA, and 200-day SMA, momentum has rolled over, ATR is high, and the $98 support level is critical. The margin compression, SBC, buybacks above quarterly FCF, acquisition/goodwill growth, and competitive pressure from Salesforce, Microsoft, and AI-native tools are real monitoring points. Net-net, I would not make this an aggressive Buy because the chart is damaged and a break below $98 could open downside toward $91 and $83–$85. But I also would not stay neutral or underweight a high-quality compounder after a 25% reset when the fundamental thesis remains intact. The right call is constructive but staged: Overweight NOW, adding gradually and letting price confirmation dictate how quickly we scale.

**Strategic Actions**: Build or increase exposure to NOW gradually rather than all at once. Start with a partial position around the current $99–$101 support area, sized at roughly one-third to one-half of the intended overweight position. Add a second tranche only if NOW reclaims $108–$109, which would show short-term trend repair. Add the final tranche above $116–$117, where the stock would be back above the VWMA and showing stronger demand through overhead supply. Keep total NOW exposure moderately above benchmark/normal weight, not a full aggressive allocation, until the chart improves. Risk control is essential: if NOW closes decisively below $98, halt additions and trim back toward neutral; if follow-through selling develops below $98, consider reducing further given downside risk toward $91 and $83–$85. On the upside, use $124–$128 as the first reassessment zone and $135–$140 as the larger recovery target where valuation and momentum should be reviewed again.
