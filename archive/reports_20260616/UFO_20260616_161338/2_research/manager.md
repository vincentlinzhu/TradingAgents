**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: Both sides agree that the space economy is a real long-term theme and that UFO remains one of the cleaner public vehicles for targeted space exposure. The bull case is strongest on the structural story: SpaceX’s IPO can validate the sector, UFO offers diversified exposure beyond one company, the 50-day average remains above the 200-day, the 200-day is still rising, RSI has reset from extreme overbought levels, and the ETF is well below its 52-week high. That argues against declaring the entire thesis broken. But the bear made the more actionable near-term argument: UFO has already had a very large move, is now below the 10-day EMA, 50-day SMA, and VWMA, MACD remains negative, RSI is weak but not washed out, volatility is elevated, and the SpaceX IPO may be shifting capital away from public proxy baskets. The key $55–56 reclaim level is still above the current price, so confirmation is missing. I do not think the evidence supports a full Sell because the long-term space thesis and long-term trend are still intact, but I also do not think traders should maintain full exposure or add before technical repair. The stronger conclusion is to reduce risk while keeping optionality: Underweight UFO until it proves the post-catalyst unwind has stabilized.

**Strategic Actions**: Trim UFO to a below-target thematic allocation rather than exiting completely. For existing positions, reduce roughly one-third to one-half of current exposure, especially if the position was built during the recent SpaceX-proxy rally. Do not add new capital while UFO remains below the $55–56 reclaim zone. Maintain only a smaller core position for long-term space-economy exposure, sized as a satellite allocation rather than a core holding. Use $50 as the near-term risk marker: if UFO breaks decisively below $50, trim further and reassess downside risk toward the mid/high $40s and the 200-day SMA near $43.6. Upgrade exposure only if UFO closes decisively above $55–56 with RSI moving back above 50 and MACD histogram improving for several sessions; a move through $58.9–61 would provide stronger confirmation that overhead supply is being absorbed.
