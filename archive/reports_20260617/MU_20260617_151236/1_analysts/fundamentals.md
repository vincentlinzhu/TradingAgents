## Company Profile

Micron Technology, Inc. is a global semiconductor company focused primarily on memory and storage products. Its core product categories include:

- DRAM
- NAND flash
- High-bandwidth memory, or HBM
- SSD and storage products
- Embedded and specialty memory for automotive, industrial, mobile, PC, and data-center markets

`MU` is highly cyclical. Its revenue and profitability are driven by memory supply-demand balances, average selling prices, capacity discipline, capital expenditure cycles, and end-market demand from data centers, AI infrastructure, PCs, smartphones, automotive electronics, and industrial systems.

Because memory is capital-intensive and commodity-like in many product categories, `MU` can experience large swings in margins and earnings. In strong cycles, operating leverage is powerful; in downturns, profitability can compress quickly.
