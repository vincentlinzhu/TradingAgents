Avoid chasing blindly at current levels. Better entry conditions:

   - Prefer confirmation from rising volume and MACD turning back above its signal line.

MU is tradable but very volatile. Use smaller position sizes. ATR near **$78** means a normal pullback can be large. Short-term traders should avoid overleveraging.

A close below **$930** would materially weaken the short-term bullish structure. A close below **$864** would suggest a larger correction is underway and would shift the setup from bullish consolidation to trend-risk mode.
