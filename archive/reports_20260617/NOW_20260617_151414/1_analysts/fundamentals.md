## Executive View

ServiceNow, Inc. `NOW` remains a high-quality enterprise software compounder with strong revenue growth, high gross margins, significant free cash flow generation, and a net-cash balance sheet. The company continues to scale its workflow automation platform across IT, employee, customer, creator/developer, and AI-enabled enterprise use cases.

However, the current fundamental picture supports a **HOLD** rather than an outright BUY. Revenue growth remains strong at roughly **22% year over year** in the latest quarter, and free cash flow is robust, but valuation is still demanding on trailing earnings, profitability margins are not expanding meaningfully on a GAAP basis, stock-based compensation remains material, and recent capital allocation has become more aggressive through large buybacks and acquisitions.

For traders, `NOW` is fundamentally attractive, but the risk/reward depends heavily on whether forward growth and margin expansion materialize. The stock looks better on forward earnings and free cash flow than on trailing GAAP earnings, but the balance of evidence suggests waiting for either a better entry price or clearer acceleration in operating leverage.

---

## Company Profile

ServiceNow, Inc. `NOW` provides a cloud-based enterprise workflow platform. Its core business is subscription software that helps large organizations digitize, automate, and manage workflows across departments. The company is best known for IT service management, but its platform has expanded into IT operations, security operations, customer service, human resources, low-code application development, and AI-powered workflow automation.

The business model is primarily subscription-based, creating recurring revenue and deferred revenue balances. This model generally supports strong cash flow, though reported working capital can appear negative because customers often pay upfront while revenue is recognized over time.

## Suggested Trading Stance

### Rating: **HOLD**

`NOW` is fundamentally strong, but the stock requires continued execution to justify its valuation. The company’s revenue growth, cash generation, and net-cash position are attractive. However, the lack of strong GAAP earnings leverage, margin compression, heavy stock-based compensation, and aggressive capital deployment argue against chasing the stock without a better setup.

### What Would Make `NOW` a BUY?

A more constructive BUY case would emerge if:

- Revenue growth remains above **20%**;
- Operating margin expands sustainably;
- Gross margin stabilizes or improves;
- Stock-based compensation declines as a percentage of revenue;
- Acquisitions contribute clearly to growth or product expansion;
- The stock trades at a more attractive FCF yield or pulls back materially.

### What Would Make `NOW` a SELL?

A SELL case would become more likely if:

- Revenue growth decelerates materially;
- Deferred revenue growth weakens;
- Gross margin continues to compress;
- Free cash flow conversion deteriorates;
- Buybacks continue to exceed free cash flow at high valuations;
- Acquisition activity fails to improve growth or margins.
