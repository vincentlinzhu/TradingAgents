## 1. Executive View

ServiceNow, Inc. (`NOW`) remains a high-quality enterprise software compounder with strong revenue growth, high recurring-revenue visibility, substantial free cash flow generation, and a net cash balance sheet. The latest quarterly data show continued top-line expansion, with Q1 2026 revenue of **$3.77B**, up approximately **22.1% year over year** from Q1 2025 revenue of **$3.09B**.

The key fundamental positives are:

- **Strong revenue growth:** Q1 2026 revenue increased more than 22% YoY.
- **Large free cash flow generation:** Q1 2026 free cash flow was **$1.53B**, and trailing four-quarter free cash flow was approximately **$4.62B** based on quarterly cash flow data.
- **Net cash position:** Cash and short-term investments of **$5.18B** exceed total debt of **$2.43B**.
- **High gross profitability:** Q1 2026 gross margin was approximately **75.1%**.
- **Shareholder returns through buybacks:** Q1 2026 repurchases were **$2.23B**, materially above prior quarters.

The key risks are:

- **Valuation still requires execution:** TTM P/E is **56.6x**, though forward P/E is much lower at **18.9x**.
- **Margin pressure:** Q1 2026 gross margin and operating margin declined versus Q1 2025.
- **Stock-based compensation remains large:** Q1 2026 SBC was **$547M**, or roughly **14.5% of revenue**.
- **Working capital/current ratio weakness:** Current ratio is **0.845**, largely due to large deferred revenue balances, but still worth monitoring.
- **Acquisition activity increased goodwill/intangibles:** Goodwill and intangible assets rose significantly, suggesting integration and capital-allocation risk.

Overall, `NOW` presents a favorable fundamental setup for traders with a medium-term horizon. The combination of revenue growth, durable SaaS economics, free cash flow strength, net cash, and an attractive forward PEG ratio supports a **BUY** stance, preferably on pullbacks or phased accumulation.

## 2. Company Profile

ServiceNow, Inc. is an enterprise cloud software company focused on digital workflow automation. The company’s platform helps large organizations digitize, automate, and optimize business processes across IT, employee workflows, customer service, security operations, creator/developer workflows, and broader enterprise service management.

The business model is primarily subscription-based SaaS, which typically creates:

- High revenue visibility.
- Large deferred revenue balances.
- Strong renewal economics.
- Operating leverage over time.
- Significant free cash flow generation.

`NOW` is positioned as a strategic enterprise software platform, not merely a point-solution vendor. This matters because platform vendors often achieve higher wallet share, better retention, and broader cross-sell opportunities.

- Q1 2026 operating margin: approximately **13.3%**

### Net Income and Net Margin

- Q1 2026 net margin: approximately **12.4%**
- Q1 2025 net margin: approximately **14.9%**

Net income growth was limited relative to revenue growth, suggesting investment spending and cost structure remain important to monitor.

### Q1 2026 Expense Breakdown

- Research and development: **$823M**
- Selling, general and administrative: **$1.50B**
  - Selling and marketing: **$1.22B**
  - General and administrative: **$288M**

R&D represented approximately **21.8% of revenue** in Q1 2026. This is substantial, but expected for a software platform investing heavily in AI, automation, product expansion, and enterprise platform capabilities.

Selling and marketing represented approximately **32.3% of revenue**, which remains high but typical for enterprise SaaS companies that rely on direct sales, large account expansion, and global go-to-market execution.

The most important question for traders is whether these investments continue to generate durable revenue growth above 20%. If growth remains strong, spending is justified. If growth decelerates materially, margin pressure could become a valuation issue.

## 7. Balance Sheet Analysis

The balance sheet remains solid, despite some areas to monitor.

### Liquidity

As of 2026-03-31:

- Cash and equivalents: **$2.70B**
- Short-term investments: **$2.48B**
- Cash and short-term investments: **$5.18B**
- Current assets: **$8.44B**
- Current liabilities: **$9.98B**
- Working capital: **negative $1.55B**
- Current ratio: **0.845**

The current ratio below 1.0 may initially look concerning. However, for SaaS companies, current liabilities often include large deferred revenue balances. Deferred revenue represents cash already collected for services to be delivered in the future, not necessarily a conventional cash repayment obligation.

Current deferred revenue was **$8.03B**, making up most of current liabilities. This reflects the subscription nature of the business and the company’s strong billings base.

### Debt and Net Cash

As of 2026-03-31:

- Total debt: **$2.43B**
- Long-term debt: **$1.49B**
- Capital lease obligations: **$940M**
- Cash and short-term investments: **$5.18B**

`NOW` therefore had approximate net cash and short-term investments of **$2.75B** after subtracting total debt.

This is a major positive. The company has balance sheet capacity to continue investing through cycles.

### Equity and Buybacks

Stockholders’ equity was **$11.73B** at Q1 2026, down from **$12.96B** at Q4 2025. Treasury stock rose sharply to **$5.38B** from **$3.05B** in the prior quarter, reflecting substantial buyback activity.

Q1 2026 repurchases were **$2.23B**, significantly above Q4 2025 repurchases of **$597M**.

This suggests management was willing to deploy capital aggressively into buybacks. For traders, this can be supportive if shares are undervalued relative to long-term fundamentals. However, buybacks must be judged against valuation and opportunity cost.

### Goodwill and Intangibles

Goodwill and intangible assets increased materially:

- Q1 2026: **$6.02B**
- Q4 2025: **$4.70B**
- Q1 2025: **$1.54B**

This increase appears related to acquisition activity. Cash flow data show Q1 2026 business purchases of **$1.33B**, following **$869M** in Q4 2025.

This creates two implications:

1. Management is actively using M&A to expand capabilities.
2. Integration risk and potential future impairment risk have increased.

- Business acquisitions: **$1.33B**

## 10. Key Fundamental Strengths

### 1. Durable Top-Line Growth

Revenue growth above 20% at `NOW`’s scale is impressive. A company generating nearly **$14B** in TTM revenue while still expanding above 20% is rare.

### 2. High Free Cash Flow Conversion

Trailing four-quarter free cash flow of approximately **$4.62B** demonstrates strong cash economics. This gives the company flexibility to fund product development, acquisitions, data-center investment, AI capabilities, and buybacks.

### 3. Net Cash Balance Sheet

Cash and short-term investments exceed total debt by approximately **$2.75B**. This reduces financial risk and gives the company optionality.

### 4. Attractive Forward Valuation Metrics

While the TTM P/E is high, the forward P/E of **18.9x** and PEG ratio of **0.98** are attractive if the company meets forward earnings expectations.

### 5. Strong Enterprise Positioning

ServiceNow’s platform-based business model supports cross-selling, high retention, and long-term enterprise relevance.

---

## 11. Key Fundamental Risks

### 1. Margin Compression

Revenue growth is strong, but Q1 2026 gross margin and net margin declined versus Q1 2025. If this trend continues, valuation could come under pressure.

### 2. High SBC

Stock-based compensation remains a major economic cost. Even with buybacks, SBC reduces the quality of reported free cash flow from a shareholder perspective.

### 3. Acquisition Integration Risk

Goodwill and intangibles increased significantly over the past year. This points to increased acquisition activity and raises the risk of integration challenges or future write-downs.

### 4. Current Ratio Below 1.0

The current ratio of **0.845** is not necessarily problematic because of deferred revenue, but traders should monitor liquidity trends, especially if buybacks and acquisitions remain elevated.

### 5. Execution Risk Embedded in Forward Valuation

The forward P/E is attractive only if expected earnings growth materializes. A slowdown in enterprise software spending, AI monetization delays, or sales-cycle elongation could hurt the stock.

## 12. Trading and Investment Implications

### Fundamental Bias: Positive

The fundamental picture supports a constructive stance on `NOW`. The company combines scale, growth, cash generation, and balance sheet strength. These are characteristics traders should favor in the software sector.

### Suggested Action

**BUY / Accumulate on weakness.**

The best tactical approach is not necessarily to chase sharp rallies, but to accumulate during pullbacks, especially if shares trade near or below major moving averages while fundamentals remain intact.

### What Would Strengthen the Buy Case

- Sustained revenue growth above 20%.
- Stable or improving gross margin.
- Expansion in operating margin.
- Continued strong free cash flow conversion.
- Buybacks that reduce diluted share count.
- Evidence that acquisitions are contributing to platform growth.

### What Would Weaken the Buy Case

- Revenue growth deceleration below high-teens levels.
- Persistent margin compression.
- Rising SBC as a percentage of revenue.
- Deterioration in free cash flow.
- Aggressive acquisitions without visible returns.
- Declining remaining performance obligations or deferred revenue growth.

---

## 13. Bottom Line

`NOW` remains fundamentally strong. The company is growing revenue at a high rate for its size, generating substantial free cash flow, maintaining a net cash position, and returning capital through buybacks. While valuation is not cheap on trailing earnings, forward valuation metrics appear reasonable for the growth profile.

The main caution is margin quality. Revenue growth is strong, but Q1 2026 showed some margin compression and SBC remains significant. Still, the company’s long-term platform position and free cash flow generation support a positive fundamental view.

**Final transaction proposal: BUY.**
