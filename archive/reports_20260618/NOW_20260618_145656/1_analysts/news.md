FINAL TRANSACTION PROPOSAL: **HOLD**

# Weekly Trading & Macro Report for `NOW`  
**Current date:** 2026-06-17  
**Instrument:** `NOW` — ServiceNow Inc.

## Executive View

`NOW` remains a high-quality enterprise software and AI workflow automation name, but the near-term setup looks balanced rather than clearly asymmetric. Recent news is supportive: `NOW` continues to deepen enterprise AI partnerships with Wipro and Cognizant, positioning itself around agentic AI deployment, AI governance, and workflow automation. These are attractive secular growth themes.

However, the stock has reportedly gained more than 16% over the past month, and the broader software sector has lagged major indices over the past six months. Competition from AI-native software platforms, valuation sensitivity, and rotation into semiconductor/AI infrastructure names remain important headwinds. Falling yields and easing geopolitical risk have recently supported growth equities, but `NOW` is still vulnerable if rates rise again or if investors rotate out of expensive software.

**Trading stance:** Maintain exposure, but avoid aggressive new buying after the recent rebound unless there is a pullback or stronger confirmation from earnings, guidance, or enterprise AI monetization metrics.

---

## 1. Key `NOW` Company-Specific Developments

### Positive: Wipro partnership targets enterprise-scale agentic AI workflows  
Recent coverage highlighted that `NOW` expanded its partnership with Wipro to deploy agentic AI workflows at scale across core enterprise functions. This is strategically important because many enterprises remain stuck between AI pilots and full production deployment. If `NOW` can become the workflow layer that converts AI experiments into operational systems, it strengthens the platform’s moat.

**Trading implication:** This supports the long-term growth case for `NOW`, especially around AI-driven workflow automation. It also reinforces the view that `NOW` is more than a traditional SaaS vendor and is trying to become a central operating layer for enterprise AI.

### Positive: Cognizant partnership focuses on AI governance  
`NOW` and Cognizant announced a partnership focused on AI governance for enterprise customers. AI governance is becoming a high-priority corporate spending area as companies deploy generative and agentic AI tools while managing compliance, model risk, auditability, data security, and operational controls.

**Trading implication:** This is a constructive vertical for `NOW` because governance and compliance workflows fit naturally into ServiceNow’s platform strengths. It may help `NOW` defend enterprise relevance even as AI-native applications emerge.

### Mixed: AI-native adoption remains a competitive threat  
One investor letter cited in recent news said `NOW` had slid earlier amid growing adoption of AI-native solutions. This highlights the market’s concern that legacy software platforms could face disruption if enterprises adopt purpose-built AI-native tools that bypass incumbent workflow vendors.

**Trading implication:** This is the key bear argument. `NOW` must show that AI increases its platform value rather than commoditizing parts of the workflow stack.

## 2. Stock & Sector Context

### `NOW` recently outperformed in a risk-on bounce  
News reports indicated `NOW` rose 4.6% in one session after yields fell and geopolitical risks eased around a reported peace deal and reopening of the Strait of Hormuz. Separately, `NOW` reportedly closed at $104.15, up 1.96% in another session and outperforming the broader market.

**Trading implication:** `NOW` remains highly sensitive to rates and risk appetite. Lower yields support long-duration software names, while rising yields could pressure valuation multiples.

### Software remains under pressure versus broader indices  
A recent software-sector article noted that the software industry has fallen 9.8% over the past six months, while the S&P 500 has returned 8.4%. That divergence matters: investors continue to favor AI infrastructure, semiconductors, and select mega-cap growth over many SaaS/software names.

**Trading implication:** Even if `NOW` is fundamentally strong, sector-level flows may cap near-term upside unless software leadership broadens.

### Comparative concern: Salesforce viewed as having an edge by some analysts  
A Zacks article compared ServiceNow and Salesforce, arguing that Salesforce may currently have an edge due to Agentforce momentum, rising earnings estimates, and lower valuation.

**Trading implication:** `NOW` may face relative valuation pressure if investors prefer cheaper AI software beneficiaries. Pair traders may consider long/short relative setups depending on valuation and earnings revision momentum.

## 3. Macro & Market Backdrop

### Equity market tone: rebound after volatility  
Global market news showed the Nasdaq and Russell 2000 rebounding after a selloff, with tech stocks climbing late in the session. However, chipmakers had recently retreated, causing pressure on the Nasdaq 100. This suggests a market still driven by AI enthusiasm but prone to sharp rotations.

**Relevance to `NOW`:**  
`NOW` is not a semiconductor company, but it trades within the broader AI/growth complex. If AI infrastructure names rally, software can benefit secondarily, but if investors focus only on chips and hardware, SaaS may lag.

### Semiconductors remain the center of AI risk appetite  
Marvell rallied sharply after positive developments around optical networking and Amazon’s Trainium chip plans. AMD and other AI infrastructure names remained in focus. This indicates that investors continue to reward companies tied directly to AI compute, networking, and chips.

**Relevance to `NOW`:**  
`NOW` must prove it has tangible AI revenue uplift, not just AI narrative exposure. The market is rewarding “hard AI infrastructure” more clearly than “AI-enabled software.”

### Rates and Fed policy remain central  
Reports referenced investors awaiting the first Federal Reserve decision under Kevin Warsh. Regardless of the specific policy details, the key trading point is that rate expectations remain central to valuation for long-duration software names.

**Relevance to `NOW`:**  
Lower yields are supportive. A hawkish Fed surprise, stronger inflation, or higher real rates would likely pressure `NOW` more than value/cyclical stocks.

### Geopolitical risk: Hormuz reopening eases energy shock fears  
News referenced a reported peace deal that could reopen the Strait of Hormuz, leading to improved market sentiment. Lower geopolitical risk and reduced oil-shock fears helped equities and lowered yields.

**Relevance to `NOW`:**  
This is indirectly positive. Lower oil-risk premiums reduce inflation fears, which can help rates fall and support growth multiples. But geopolitical headlines can reverse quickly.

## 4. Bull Case for `NOW`

1. **Enterprise AI workflow leader:** `NOW` is well positioned to become a core platform for agentic AI deployment in large organizations.  
2. **Partnership ecosystem expanding:** Wipro and Cognizant relationships improve go-to-market reach and implementation credibility.  
3. **AI governance tailwind:** Enterprises need governance, compliance, audit, and controls around AI, which aligns with ServiceNow’s workflow strengths.  
4. **Lower yields support valuation:** If rates continue to fall, high-quality software multiples could expand.  
5. **Strong recent momentum:** A reported 16%+ monthly gain shows renewed investor interest.

---

## 5. Bear Case for `NOW`

1. **Valuation risk:** After a strong monthly rally, upside may be less attractive without a pullback.  
2. **Software sector underperformance:** SaaS/software has lagged the S&P 500 materially over six months.  
3. **AI-native competition:** New AI-first platforms could pressure incumbent workflow vendors.  
4. **Relative competition from CRM and others:** Some analysts see Salesforce as better positioned near term due to valuation and Agentforce momentum.  
5. **Rate sensitivity:** A hawkish Fed or higher yields would likely hurt `NOW`.

---

## 6. Trading Strategy for `NOW`

### Recommended stance: **HOLD**

`NOW` has a constructive long-term AI workflow story, but the stock’s recent strength and sector headwinds reduce the attractiveness of chasing the rally. Existing investors can continue holding, but new capital should wait for either a technical pullback or stronger evidence that AI partnerships are converting into revenue growth and margin expansion.

### Actionable levels / triggers to monitor

- **Add / Buy trigger:** Pullback with stable fundamentals, especially if driven by macro noise rather than company-specific deterioration.  
- **Bullish confirmation:** Upward revisions to subscription revenue growth, operating margin guidance, remaining performance obligations, or AI product adoption metrics.  
- **Reduce / Sell trigger:** Evidence that AI-native competitors are displacing `NOW` workflows, deterioration in enterprise IT budgets, or sharp multiple compression from higher yields.  
- **Macro trigger:** If yields fall further and software leadership broadens, `NOW` could resume outperformance.  
- **Hedge idea:** For holders concerned about Nasdaq volatility, consider hedging with QQQ/IGV exposure or pairing `NOW` against a cheaper enterprise software peer depending on relative valuation.

---

## 7. Bottom Line

`NOW` remains a premium enterprise software name with credible AI exposure through agentic workflow automation and AI governance. The Wipro and Cognizant partnerships strengthen the long-term narrative. Still, after recent gains and amid ongoing software-sector underperformance, the risk/reward is not compelling enough for an aggressive buy at this point.

**Final view:** `NOW` is a **HOLD** — keep exposure, but wait for a better entry or clearer earnings-driven confirmation before adding aggressively.
