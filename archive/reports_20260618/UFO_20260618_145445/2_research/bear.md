## 9. The ETF structure cuts both ways

If they want broad growth, they can buy stronger themes with better momentum.

- Less direct than SpaceX
- Less dominant than AI/semi leadership
- Less defensive than traditional aerospace/defense
- Still exposed to volatile second-order space names
- Still dependent on thematic sentiment and ETF flows

The ETF structure reduces single-name risk, yes. But it also dilutes exposure to the strongest winners and leaves holders exposed to broad thematic risk.
