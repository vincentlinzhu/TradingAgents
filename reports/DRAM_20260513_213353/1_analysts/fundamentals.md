DRAM is not a conventional corporation; it is an ETF. Its purpose is likely to provide investors with thematic exposure to companies involved in memory semiconductors, such as DRAM, NAND flash, high-bandwidth memory, and related supply-chain businesses.

Because DRAM is an ETF:

- It does **not** report operating margins, net income, or free cash flow like a company.
- Its valuation metrics are derived from the underlying holdings.
- Its performance depends on the aggregate movement of the companies in the ETF portfolio.

For traders, DRAM should be analyzed more like a **sector/thematic exposure vehicle** than a standalone business.
