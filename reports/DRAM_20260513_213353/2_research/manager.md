**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: The bull side made a credible case that DRAM is tied to a real AI infrastructure bottleneck: high-bandwidth memory, DRAM/NAND demand, AI server memory intensity, and broadening semiconductor capex. DRAM has also become a very liquid, visible vehicle for that theme, and the tape has not technically broken: price is still near highs, above the 10-day EMA/VWMA, MACD remains positive, and the May 12 pullback was quickly bought. That said, the bear side carried the risk/reward argument. DRAM has nearly doubled in roughly six weeks, trades near the $56.38 high, remains overbought with RSI above 70, sits well above short-term trend support, has elevated daily volatility, and carries a high trailing P/E around 43x for a cyclical memory-linked basket. The massive inflows are supportive while they continue, but they also create crowding and potential mechanical selling risk if sentiment turns. The AI memory theme is real, but at the current price the setup depends on continued perfect momentum, flows, and narrative strength. That is not enough margin of safety for fresh capital. Because the trend is still intact, this is not a Sell; but the better call is to reduce risk rather than chase.

**Strategic Actions**: Trim DRAM exposure rather than exit completely. For existing positions, reduce to a below-target or half-sized tactical allocation, taking profits into strength near the current $54–$56 area. Do not add new capital at current levels unless the trader is running a very short-term momentum mandate. Use strict risk controls on the remaining position: an aggressive stop below $51.30, a firmer de-risking trigger below $50, and a near-full exit trigger on a break below $48.32 or a MACD bearish crossover with expanding downside volume. Reconsider adding only if DRAM consolidates for several weeks, resets toward $48–$49 and holds support, or if it breaks above $56.38 on genuinely strong volume while flows and underlying memory names remain healthy. Until then, prioritize capital preservation over chasing the AI memory narrative.
