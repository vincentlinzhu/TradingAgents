RVI may keep trading higher. I am not denying that. Momentum can overshoot, especially in a retail-driven thematic vehicle.

But as an investment, the setup is weak because the current price reflects a lot of optimism while the underlying evidence remains incomplete.

At current levels, RVI has:

- Rallied roughly **78.8%** in less than a month  
- Faded sharply from the **$48.78** intraday high  
- Printed a high-volume upper wick  
- Reached overbought RSI conditions before the latest surge  
- Moved far above short-term trend references  
- Produced only thin, indirect company-specific news  
- Provided no available NAV, financial statements, holdings, fees, or valuation methodology  
- Become highly dependent on retail enthusiasm, AI sentiment, IPO optimism, and macro liquidity  

My stance:

- **Existing holders:** tighten stops and consider taking partial profits.  
- **New investors:** do not chase at current levels.  
- **Watch $42.00:** a break signals momentum cooling.  
- **Watch $39.13:** a close below this level seriously damages the breakout thesis.  
- **Demand NAV, holdings, fees, and valuation disclosure before treating RVI as a real investment rather than a momentum trade.**

Bull, momentum plus scarcity can move a stock. I agree.

But momentum is not due diligence. Scarcity is not valuation. Distribution is not a NAV floor. Retail demand is not a margin of safety.

After a near-80% rally in an opaque fund-like vehicle, the burden of proof is on the bulls — and right now, the proof is still not there.
