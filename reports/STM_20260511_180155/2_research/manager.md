**Recommendation**: Underweight

**Rationale**: Both sides agree STM has a much better business story than it had at the trough: the LEO satellite revenue ramp is real, the balance sheet is strong, Q1 revenue grew 23% year over year, gross margin has improved from the 2025 lows, and the technical trend is undeniably strong. The bull case is strongest on the idea that STM is being rerated before the earnings recovery fully appears, with LEO, edge sensing, auto/industrial recovery, and power semis supporting future operating leverage. That said, the bear made the more compelling risk/reward argument for the current price. STM is at $60+, near its 52-week high, after a roughly 52% one-month move and 109% YTD gain, while Q1 profitability remains thin: 3.1% operating margin, 1.2% net margin, $0.04 EPS, negative free cash flow, and elevated inventory. The forward valuation may be reasonable if the $2.23 EPS recovery arrives, but that bridge is still execution-dependent, and the latest quarter was not clean sequentially, with revenue and margins down from Q4. The conclusion is not that STM is broken or that the long-term story should be dismissed; it is that the stock has moved faster than the financial proof. I would not chase here, and I would manage existing exposure more cautiously until the company confirms margin expansion, FCF conversion, and inventory normalization.

**Strategic Actions**: Trim STM exposure into current strength rather than adding at $60+. For accounts with an existing full position, reduce toward a smaller-than-benchmark or half-sized position and protect the remainder with a trailing stop or a hard risk level below the first support zone. Do not initiate new aggressive buys at current levels. Reassess around $55–$56 for tactical support, but only add selectively if price stabilizes and market conditions remain constructive. A deeper pullback toward $52–$53 or the prior breakout zone near $49–$50.50 would offer a more attractive risk/reward, especially if accompanied by evidence of improving gross/operating margin, positive free cash flow, and stable or declining inventory. If STM breaks out above $62.50–$65 on strong volume, avoid chasing with size; keep any momentum exposure small and tightly risk-managed. Upgrade the stance only if the next results confirm that the LEO and recovery thesis is translating into EPS and cash flow, not just revenue narrative.
