## 3. Key Macro Forces Affecting `UFO`

### Interest rates and long-duration growth sensitivity

`UFO` is likely sensitive to discount-rate expectations because many space-economy businesses are valued on future growth potential rather than near-term free cash flow. If rates remain elevated or real yields rise, long-duration thematic assets tend to face valuation pressure.

**Trading read:**  
`UFO` benefits from falling yields and easier financial conditions. It is vulnerable if bond yields rise or if central banks signal tighter policy for longer.

### Defense, security, and satellite infrastructure demand

Space infrastructure increasingly overlaps with national security, communications, surveillance, weather monitoring, and broadband connectivity. Even if consumer risk appetite weakens, government and defense-related spending can provide a more stable demand channel for some space-related companies.

**Trading read:**  
Geopolitical tension can be a double-edged sword. It may support satellite, defense, and space-infrastructure demand, but it can also trigger broader risk-off moves that hurt thematic ETFs.

### Technology and AI spillover

AI, automation, robotics, and satellite data analytics are increasingly connected to space-economy use cases. This week’s broader interest in robotics, AI, and “physical AI” themes could be indirectly supportive for `UFO` if investors broaden their search beyond mega-cap AI names.

**Trading read:**  
If AI/thematic leadership broadens, `UFO` could benefit. If AI enthusiasm narrows or fades, `UFO` may struggle.
