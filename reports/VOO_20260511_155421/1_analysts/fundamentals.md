## Valuation Analysis

### P/E Ratio: 29.34

`VOO` currently has a trailing P/E ratio of **29.34**. This indicates that investors are paying nearly 29 times trailing earnings for the underlying earnings stream of the S&P 500.

This is a relatively elevated valuation level for a broad-market equity ETF. A high P/E ratio can be justified if:

- earnings growth remains strong,
- interest rates decline or remain supportive,
- profit margins stay elevated,
- large-cap technology and growth stocks continue leading the index.

However, it also means `VOO` may be more vulnerable to:

- earnings disappointments,
- higher-for-longer interest rates,
- valuation compression,
- risk-off equity rotations.

### Price-to-Book Ratio: 1.74

The reported price-to-book ratio for `VOO` is **1.74**. This is not directly comparable to a single operating company because `VOO` holds a portfolio of companies. However, it gives a broad view of how the market values the underlying asset base.

The P/B ratio appears less aggressive than the P/E ratio, but for an ETF like `VOO`, earnings multiple and free-cash-flow quality of the underlying companies are usually more important than book value.

### Dividend Yield: 1.08%

`VOO` has a dividend yield of **1.08%**. This is relatively modest and indicates that `VOO` is primarily a capital appreciation vehicle rather than an income-focused investment.

For traders and investors, the low yield means total return depends largely on price appreciation rather than income generation.
